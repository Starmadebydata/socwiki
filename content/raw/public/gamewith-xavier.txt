This enables more frequent use of [Falling Comet Dash], making the character effective in battles against multiple enemies. Clash Defender A RK 11 Xavier can one shot the entire enemy team when he is set as a Defender in your PvP/Clash Lineup. By combining [Spear of Iria] and [Falling Commet Dash], Xavier will always take his turn before the enemy, and if the attacking player places his units too close to one another, Xavier can sweep their entire team with a well-placed [Falling Commet Dash] . Equip Double-Sided Halberd To maximize Xavier's one shot teamwipe potential, equip Xavier with the Double-Sided Halberd as well as The Magician Tarot. Both of these equips synergize well with AoE attacks and will help Xavier reach the enemy HP threshold for that satisfying one shot. Physical Glass Cannon Xavier has very high offensive potential, but his defensive stats and HP are sorely lacking for him to be viable in high-level content. This is because due to the fact that maximizing Xavier's offensive potential means sacrificing a portion of his defensive stats with [Risky Move]. This implies that Xavier is best played as a sweeper. This way, you can eliminate multiple enemies while giving Xavier the best chance to survive for his next turns. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Xavier Aim to Reach 2★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 2★ → 5★ Star upgrades don’t increase the AoE damage from Xavier’s trait—they only boost his attack power when enemies are nearby. While this bonus is fairly strong, it doesn’t drastically change how he plays, so his upgrade priority is on the lower side. You should consider upgrading him based on how often you use him in your team. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Xavier Double-Sided Halberd Army-breaking Spear The Silent Guard For offense, [Double-Sided Halberd] is recommended. Defensive alternatives include [The Silent Guard] and [Pendant Halberd] are recommended. Recommended Trinkets for Xavier Maverick's Cloak Evergreen Pendent Shield of Justice When focusing on AoE attacks, [Berserk Incense] is recommended. If focusing on durability, [Mystery Guardian] is recommended as it has good synergy with the Parry effect from [Musou]. Also, [Spring Pill] is viable since it supplements Xavier's survivability. Recommended Tarots for Xavier Dream of The Magician Silence of The Hermit Temptation of The Devil If you're prioritizing AoE damage, go with [The Magician]; if you're aiming for durability, choose [The Hermit] or [The Tower]. Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . A young military leader in the King's Army of Iria, and an earnest soldier who serves Dantalion and Iria with his outstanding combat skills. After losing his parents in war, Xavier became a ward of the kingdom and received the support and training needed to become a top-notch soldier. This upbringing has ingrained a fierce loyalty to Iria in him, and he consideres defending its peace to be his top priority. Xavier is ready and willing to lay his life down for his country.
