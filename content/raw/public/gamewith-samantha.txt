Be sure to utilize it, as it makes even Skills with long CDs easier to use. Auto-Healer Samantha's defining characteristic is her ability to heal nearby allies out of her turn. As long as Samantha is near an ally that takes enough DMG, she can spend 1 NRG to heal these allies. This Trait combined with her RK 1 Skill [NRG Restoration], Samantha is guaranteed to heal at least two allies who were Injured while she was nearby. AoE Purge and Dispel At RK 7, Samantha can unlock [Light of Celestial Purge]. This grants Samantha the ability to remove buffs from enemies while simultaneously removing debuffs from nearby allies. This can be a powerful tool if Samantha is positioned properly and used at the right time. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Samantha Aim to Reach 2★ → 3★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 2★ → 3★ At 2-stars, Samantha gains an extra heal, and at 3-stars, she gains unlimited heals. If you’re planning to include Samantha in your main lineup, definitely aim for 2-3 star level ups. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Samantha Reorganized Cube Diffusing Prism Focus Wand For healers in general, [Reorganized Cube] is almost always the best choice. However, if you also want Samantha to participate in attacks, [Focus Wand] to extend the Range of [Light of Judgement], or [Diffusing Prism] to increase AoE damage. Recommended Trinkets for Samantha Old Scrolls of Auguste Disaster Bottle Roaring Flint Samantha's primary role as a healer means her Trinkets should support allies or protect herself. Recommended Tarots for Samantha Mercy of The High Priestess Elegance of the Empress Dream of The Magician For healing, [The High Priestess] and [The Empress] are recommended. If you want Samantha to participate in offense, use [The Magician] for AoE focus, or [The Sun] for single-target focus. Recommended Engravings for Samantha Resonance Effect Cup Cup Increases Healing Effect by 10% and Healing Received by 10% . Wand Cup At the end of the turn, there is a 50% chance to gain [Engraving Resonance]. Effect: When using an [active skill] next time, refunds the NRG consumed and resets the CD. The skill effect has a CD of 4 turns. (Does not trigger in real-time combat. The effect changes to increasing HP, ATK and DEF by 5%) Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, [Wand + Cup] is highly recommended since Samantha's playstyle mainly revolves around active skills and consuming NRG. For that reason, [Wand + Cup] can greatly help Samantha store NRG for her passive heals. Alternatively, you could also engrave [Cup + Cup] if you feel like her heals are somewhat lacking. A kind woman and devout maidservant of Radiant. Samantha worked tirelessly in the town of Convallaria during the war, helping to heal the sick and wounded. This led to her becoming a beloved figure to the local children. Adopted by the Sanctuary as an orphan, her outstanding talent quickly set her apart from her peers and caught the eye of the Hierophant himself who adopted her and ensured she received the best education. As an adult, Samantha has proven him right to have done so, becoming a Judge who carries out his missions. However, Samantha's time in Iria has led to a conflict between her heart and her faith.
