He stacks lots of HP on himself, and converts it to P.ATK, meaning that increased survivability equates to more offensive output. If you enjoy having a powerful and deadly, self-sustaining wall, Camelot is the character for you. Predator's Law Trait Camelot’s trait, [Predator’s Law], increases his max HP by 30-50% at the start of the battle, and reduces percentage-based damage by 15-35%. He also ignores [Block] effects on enemies and generates 20% life steal, and at 3-star, he gains [Toughness], meaning he ignores any stat drops at lower HP levels. At the start of the battle, Camelot increases his own P.ATK by 9-15% of his (boosted) max HP, and has access to [Declaration of Conquest] (“Instant. Deals 30% physical AoE DMG to the designated target. After the attack, applies 6 stacks of [Coercion] to the target for 2 turns. Character gains [Disdain] for 4 turns, and gains 30% of the characters health as a [Physical Shield].”]. This skill has a range of 5 tiles, and a CD of 4 turns, but uses no NRG. [Coercion] is a unique debuff that causes the target to lose both ATK and DEF, the fixed amount being 1% of Camelot’s max HP (or 0.5% for Union characters) per stack, and the effect cannot be dispelled or immunized. [Disdain] is a unique buff for Camelot that increases the amount of damage he deals to targets with [Coercion] by 10% (30% if the target(s) has 6 stacks). If Camelot is attacked by a target with 6 stacks of [Coercion], he gains back 35% of his max HP after the attack, once per round. This buff cannot be dispelled or immunized. When Camelot uses an active AoE attack, he applies 1-3 stacks of [Coercion] to all targets for 2 turns, and, if a target already has [Coerciom] he gets to [Act Again], but also cannot do so again for another 4-2 rounds (depending on star level). This means that he can gain a bonus action every other turn at 5-star. When gaining [Act Again] through his trait, he also gains 1 NRG at 5-star (once per round), and after the first active AoE attack each round, he gains a physical shield worth 10-20% of his max HP. Skill Recommendations Crushing Basic Attack [Crushing] is the preferred choice. This skill is a 3x2 AoE, similar to Magnus’ basic attack, but it deals 120% AoE DMG, which decreases by 15% (down to 90% minimum) per target hit. Targets with 6 layers of [Coercion] always take the full 120% DMG, though. The first target in each column also gets knocked back by 1 tile. This skill is very good for Camelot, since some of his CDs are quite long, and this attack is strong enough in its own right that it is worth using on turns when you cannot use his main offensive skill(s). As an added bonus, this skill choice also gives Camelot another 3% base HP when you select it on the skill tree. King's Fighting Spirit Reaction Skill [King’s Fighting Spirit] reduces physical damage taken by 15%, and, upon taking damage (from attacks, whether active or not, but not from passive damage sources like infection or sacrificing HP via Credenza’s trinket, etc.), Camelot restores 30% of his HP and gets an additional 1,000 SPD, allowing him to instantly act again (or continue his action, if it procs either right before his normal turn or due to a counterattack, etc. during his normal turn). This effect had a CD of 5 turns, but this skill pretty much speaks for itself in its usefulness; more turns equate to more damage. Verdict - Solo Carry Skill 1 Camelot’s main offensive skill, [Verdict - Solo Carry] is reminiscent of Auguste’s [Wrath - Swift] skill, in that it is a 3-in-1 skill, consisting of an [Instant] skill and two regular skills that get cycled through in order as they are used. The first skill, [Verdict - Solo Carry], is an [Instant] skill that allows Camelot to jump to a tile within 3 tiles of himself and deal 40% AoE DMG to all targets within a 1-tile radius of where he lands, and then he inflicts them with [⦸ Reaction Skills] for 2 turns. This skill then turns into [Verdict - Army’s Fall], which is a 3x3 AoE in front of Camelot that deals 100% AoE DMG to all targets, and inflicts [Isolation] on them for 2 turns, which renders them unable to benefit from any aura effects. Finally, the skill transforms into [Verdict - World Conquest], which allows Camelot to jump to a tile within 3 tiles of himself and deals 170% AoE DMG to all targets within 3 tiles of where he lands (this gets reduced by 15% per target hit, down to 110%, but any targets with 6 stacks of [Coercion] take full damage). Any targets in the 1-tile radius around where he lands get stunned for 1 turn, as well. The skill then reverts to [Verdict - Solo Carry]. All three of these skills cost only 1 NRG, and the entire suite of skills has a 3-turn CD once he uses the final skill. This skill is the method by which Camelot does the majority of his damage, and, as such, reducing its CD with Credenza is an excellent way to get the most out of him offensively. Scorn the Masses Skill 2 Camelot’s passive, [Scorn the Masses], gives him an extra 15% max HP at the beginning of the battle, and also heals his HP by 15% at the start of his turn. When he is attacked by an enemy whose current HP is lower than his own current HP, he becomes immune to any [Bound], [Disruption], or knockback effects. Additionally, before he is attacked by any single-target attacks, he dispels any dispellable [Shield Break] effects on the attacker. Since he relies on high HP and shields to keep himself safe and do increased damage, this passive is an integral part of his kit, with some added utility to keep him safe from pesky debuffs and knockback. Proud Lion Heart Skill 3 Camelot’s leader aura, [Proud Lion Heart], provides Union characters on the team the usual 10% ATK and 20% DEF buff, as well as, when they are in a [Healthy] state, providing them an extra 10% DMG boost, plus a 10% ATK boost if the character has a physical shield worth at least 30% of their own max HP. This synergizes perfectly with Camelot’s own kit, and with plenty of other Union characters, since shielding is common among the Union. Equipment For a weapon, the best choice is Camelot’s Astral Imprint, Surtr the Darkfire Axe, as it provides a significant amount of extra HP, and gives the wielder an ATK buff and a unique physical AoE DMG buff whenever they give themselves a physical shield, which Camelot does very frequently. Other great options include Feast Axe, Brutal Axe, or Hammer “Dragon Rise”. For a trinket, you can either opt for Shield of Justice, which works beautifully with the shields in his kit, or Roaring Flint, for additional stacks of [Coercion] on multiple targets. Outside of this, Origin Hourglass is always a great choice or, because he loves having lots of HP, you can also use Crystal Burst Armor on him. For a tarot, Magician will represent the best and most accessible offensive option, while Fool’s will translate to still-good damage, with more survivability (which is good on a bruiser like Camelot). Devil (or even Justice) can also work well on him. Sword + Wand is the best engraving option, as (since I can already see this coming) double Pentacle will translate to too much loss of damage, since its HP boosts over Sword + Wand are only base HP boosts, and will not translate to a significant enough boost to compensate for the lost damage output from the 12% in-battle ATK boost (plus better attack rolls) of Sword + Wand. Remember, Camelot is not a tank—he is a DPS bruiser, so if he is not doing enough damage, he is not doing his main job. When rolling stats. I suggest prioritizing HP, with P.ATK being a secondary priority. Additional Notes Camelot has multiple ways to gain additional actions, so he should be going frequently (even more so if you feed into him with other [Act Again] skills). As such, he very much benefits from Credenza, since his CDs can keep him from fully enjoying the extra turns he receives (his basic attack is great, but it is much more satisfying to use his [Verdict] skills than his basic). And do not forget that, especially once he reaches 3-star, it is okay for him to take hits, as it will not interfere with his damage output, between [Toughness] and all of his self-healing.
