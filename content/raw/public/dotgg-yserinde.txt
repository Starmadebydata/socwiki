She also has the advantage of being able to function well at very low investment levels, meaning that for those with cramped shard mines, she can still excel without her stars. Will of Blood Plume Trait Yserinde’s trait, [Will of Blood Plume], gives her [Jump] and [Unyielding] (which allows her to survive a fatal attack once), and, when her [Resolve] activates and keeps her from dying, she gains [Deflection] (“Buff. Reduces damage or piercing damage taken by 50% when receiving the next instance of non-DoT damage. Removed after taking effect.”). Additionally, she can jump over other allies, enemies, neutral characters, and items. Her basic attacks can also target adjacent enemies, and all enemies on the battlefield are passively affected by her [Everfeather] aura, which causes any [Black Feather] stacks to not be consumed upon use (meaning she pairs well with Nydia). Yserinde is also provided with an active skill, [Plume Stratagem], which places a single [Blood Plume Blade] on a chosen empty tile within 6 tiles of herself. This skill is [Instant] and costs no NRG on its first use, but for the following 2 turns, it will cost 2 NRG and not be instant. Then, on the 4th turn, it will return to being [Instant] and free, and this will continue to cycle throughout the battle. [Blood Plume Blade] is a placable object that, when any character stays on its tile (whether to use a skill or ending their turn on it), Yserinde inflicts [Black Feather] for 2 turns and [Frostbite] (“Life Loss. At the end of the turn, loses HP equal to 50% of the sum of the caster’s P.ATK and M.ATK.”) for 1 turn to all enemies within 2 tiles, while also dealing piercing DMG equal to 50% of Yserinde’s P.ATK, and then gives Yserinde [Deflection]. Her trait also gives her an extra 10-40% P.DEF, and adds 10-30% of her P.DEF to her P.ATK, and further reduces the damage or piercing DMG taken by attacks by 40-60% when [Deflection] takes effect. Starting at 3-star, whenever a [Blood Plume Blade] is activated, Yserinde gains 1 NRG. Starting at 4-star, when Yserinde has [Endless Web] (to be discussed under Skill 1), her piercing DMG and DoT DMG increases by 10-25%. Skill Recommendations Hypnotic Attack Basic Attack Both basic attacks have a range of 2-3, which gets extended to 1-3 by her trait, and both have underwhelming secondary effects, so ultimately the choice of basic is up to each player. I personally suggest [Hypnotic Attack], since the 30% chance to inflict [Sleep] is more widely useful than the rare instance in which you might need to thwart an enemy’s Dodge attempts. Either way, her basic attack is not overly important. Silent Wing Reaction Skill [Silent Wing] is Yserinde’s unique reaction skill, which activates when she is attacked and triggering [Deflection]. This skill causes her to dispel 1 debuff from herself and gain [Armor of Ice - Extra] for 2 turns. This buff is similar to Simona’s [Armor of Ice], and even counts as it for purposes of stacking; it provides a physical shield worth 30% of her max HP, makes her immune to frosty tile effects, and causes her to inflict a small movement debuff on enemies when they attack her with melee attacks. Overall, this just increases her tankiness, which is already considerable, due to the stacking damage reduction between her trait and [Deflection]. Endless Web Skill 1 [Endless Web] is the only active skill in the build I am suggesting for Yserinde. This skill, when used, locks onto the 3 closest enemies to Yserinde within 6 tiles of her, and creates 1 [Blood Plume Blade] on an adjacent tile next to each enemy. If there are not enough valid targets, she instead places 3 [Blood Plume Blades] adjacent to the 1 closest enemy target. After she uses this skill, she then creates an [Endless Web] aura effect, which does as follows: “[Aura] Before all allies in battle attack, if they have [Deflection] or the target has [Black Feather], increases DMG, piercing DMG and DoT effects by 15% and can generate 30% [Life Steal]. These effects last until before the start of the next turn. Additionally grants all Union allies [Endless Web - Frost] (‘After attacking, if they have a [physical shield] of 30% or more HP, inflicts 1 [Minor Frostbite] (which is the same as [Frostbite], but with a 15% modifier instead of 50%) for 1 turn, limited to 1 time per enemy per round.’).” As long as she has the aura active (which it will be until the start of the third turn after usage), she cannot use the skill again, so even though it has no native CD, it effectively has a CD of 3 turns. The skill costs 4 NRG. Overall, [Endless Web] is an excellent way to quickly get a number of [Blood Plume Blades] on the battlefield, and ready to use. Plume Rend Skill 2 [Plume Rend] is a passive which activates after every 4th placement or trigger of a [Blood Plume Blade] (not including the effects of this skill), and causes piercing DMG equal to 50% of Yserinde’s P.ATK to all enemies within 2 tiles of any [Blood Plume Blade] or targets that have [Black Feather] on the battlefield. This counts as triggering [Blood Plume Blade], but does not count toward the next use of this passive. This can be activated on the first turn by placing one Plume with [Plume Stratagem], followed by activating [Endless Web], so she can function quite well even at the start of a fight. Royal Guard Escort Skill 3 [Royal Guard Escort] causes Yserinde to, whenever an ally triggers a [Blood Plume Blade] by stopping on it, give that ally [Armor of Ice - Extra] for 2 turns, plus [Deflection] and [Move Again], allowing them to finish out whatever remaining movement they might still have leftover. This works great for characters like Marcille and Credenza who want to execute [Standby] as much as possible, and obviously having the ability to grant allies additional tankiness by way of shields and [Deflection] is quite welcome. Additionally, this passive causes [Plume Rend] to count allies with [Deflection] as [Blood Plume Blades] for the purposes of where it unleashes its AoEs. This basically means that, with proper planning, your characters can be turned into mobile Plumes that you can position near enemies to cause additional damage when [Plume Rend] activates! Equipment For a weapon, I suggest Newborn Blade, given that it is easy to access, provides a big ATK boost, and makes her even tankier (both increasing her max HP and the size of shields on herself). Nocturnal Glow gives her both an ATK and DEF boost, which both help her DoTs and piercing, and tankiness, so you can opt for that route, as well. The other options are less desirable (Blade of Stratagem is only there for the P.ATK boost for her piercing DMG, and Guardian’s Will is only there for increased tanking with shields). For a trinket, her best option is her Astral Imprint, Crimson Quill, which causes her to, when she creates a Plume, increase her ATK and DEF and (at at least 2-star) restore NRG. Otherwise, she can use either a Shield of Justice or Maverick’s Cloak for the ATK and DEF boosts they provide. For a tarot, I personally like Devil, since it plays into all the parts of her kit, but Hanged Man or Death can also work very well on her, so honestly whichever one you have the best rolls on might be your best option. For engravings, Sword + Wand is best for increased ATK and DEF, and when rolling for stats, prioritize P.ATK, with a secondary focus on P.DEF. Additional Notes Phantom Shift Alt. Skill While it is not necessarily going to be making it into her kit on a regular basis, [Phantom Shift] is a noteworthy skill which is actually 2 skills in one. The first option is [Phantom - Falling Feather], which is an [Instant] skill that teleports Yserinde to a [Blood Plume Blade] within 4 tiles of herself (which will then give her all the relevant effects of landing on a Plume). The second option is [Phantom - Swift Feather], which is an [Instant] skill that allows Yserinde to swap places with any allied or enemy target, and grant them [Deflection] if they are an ally. Using either skill puts this skill on CD for 3 turns and then also refreshes the ability to [Instant] use [Plume Stratagem] at no NRG cost. As I said, this skill may not be overly useful in most content (compared to her passives), but it is highly unique, and will likely eventually have its moment. Essentially, the way I envision using her is quite simple; on turn 1, use [Plume Stratagem], followed by [Endless Web]. Then, on turns 2 and 3, use [Plume Stratagem]. Starting on turn 4, rinse and repeat that 3-turn rotation. Her excellent Seeker mobility combined with the mobility enhancements in her trait (her jumping shenanigans, mostly) will allow her to position herself easily to place Plumes exactly where you want them, and her tankiness will allow her to take hits that few other characters can endure.
