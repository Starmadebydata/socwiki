She is a unique support who consumes her own HP when using Skills, and gains additional benefits by consuming a large amount of HP or keeping her HP below a certain threshold . Since she has healing and CD reduction, she shines when combined with powerful Attackers. Manage Divine Revelation Stacks Credenza's Skills are enhanced whenever she has the [Divine Revelation] buff, gaining powerful additional effects. Since stacks can be obtained by increasing her Star Level or through some Skill effects, it's important to decide how to generate Divine Revelation and which Skills to use when forming your team . Divine Revelation Mechanics How to Obtain Divine Revelation 1 Acquired at the start of an action if you have the [Pray for Divine Revelation] buff How to obtain Pray for Divine Revelation - At 5-stars, at the start of battle - Use [Hymn of Absolution] - Use [Hierophany] - Acquired with [Trial of Radiance] when not possessing Divine Revelation/Pray for Divine Revelation 2 Acquired via Skill effects - When [Trial of Radiance] is equipped, acquired at the start of an action if HP is below 70% 3 Sacrifice 20% HP on Standby to acquire You can obtain [Divine Revelation] through specific Skill effects, Star Level Ups, or on [Standby]. With the effect of the signature Trinket , you can gain a reposition effect, allowing you to easily obtain Divine Revelation, so if you want to make the most of Credenza, try to get it. Divine Revelation Effects 1 Enhances some Skills [Blazing Benediction]: Changes to an [Instant] Skill [Benevolent Light]: Recovers target's NRG by 2 + adds CD -2 [Graceful Salvation]: Adds [△ATK II] & [△DMG II] [Hymn of Absolution]: Adds [Radiant Grace] buff 2 When activating an additional effect, adds healing equal to 30% of M.ATK 3 5-stars only, NRG cost -1 Pt when possessing [Divine Revelation] Powerful, Single-use Trait Skill By consuming HP or taking damage a certain number of times, the Trait Skill [Hierophany] can be activated once per battle . It's a very powerful support Skill that provides various effects like healing, buffs, and CD reduction to surrounding allies, so make sure to use it. Hierophany Mechanics How to Use Hierophany [Hierophany] can be used by accumulating 12 stacks, which are gained by consuming 10% HP or by taking damage . If you equip the Passive Skill [Hierophany's Return], it can be used up to twice per battle, allowing you to use [Hierophany] consecutively. Effects of Hierophany 1 Can be activated a maximum of once per battle. Equipping [Hierophany's Return] increases the number of possible activations by 1. 2 Prepares for 1 Turn, then dispels all debuffs from all allies within a 5-Tile radius and restores 100% HP. Additionally, applies all Lv.2 Buffs that last for 2 Turns. During preparation, the effect activates once immediately, but the healing amount and buff durations are halved. Additionally, you gain [Prayer for Divine Revelation]. 3 2-stars or higher: Additionally restores 1 Pt of allies' NRG. 4 4-stars or higher: Reduces all skill cooldowns by 1 and grants the [Radiant Grace] buff. Reduces Ally CD Credenza, through [Hierophany] and [Benevolent Light], is characterized by her ability to grant the valuable CD reduction effect to allies. By combining her with powerful Attackers who have main Skills with long CDs, such as SP Samantha and Kvare , you can gain a significant advantage in battle . Needs NRG Support Although she has many support skills with no cooldown, Credenza’s own NRG consumption is quite high. Additionally, depending on her HP consumption and skill setup, she may need to keep her HP low, which tends to reduce her consistency. Furthermore, since her Move and SPD are low, there are situations where Credenza herself requires support. It’s important to consider the party composition for each stage so that Credenza can focus on providing support. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Credenza Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ 3-stars is a good stopping point since it grants a CD reduction effect to [Hierophany]. If you want to make Credenza even easier to use, you definitely want to get her to 5-stars, which grants [Divine Revelation] at the start of battle, as well as reduces NRG consumption whenever she has the buff. Utilize the Resonant Crystal's Skill to acquire Divine Revelation, and reduce your allies' Skill CDs with [Benevolent Light] and [Hierophany]. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Benevolent Light ★★★★★ This is a powerful support Skill that reduces CDs, which is one of Credenza's signature features, so it should be a top priority for your team. It also has an NRG Recovery effect, so use it in combination with characters that have high NRG consumption or characters whose main skills have a long CD. Radiant Barrier ★★★★★ A Reaction that reduces single-target damage taken by nearby allies. It consumes Credenza's HP, so if used effectively, you can reduce the time until you can use [Hierophany] and gain Divine Revelation. Merciful Strike ★★★★★ Although you won't use Basic Attacks often, you should generally equip this Basic Attack because it provides a healing increase effect. Graceful Salvation ★★★★ An excellent Skill that heals nearby allies at the start of the turn and also grants an ATK & Damage Dealt UP buff if you have [Divine Revelation]. Be careful when equipping it with [Trial of Radiance] as it also heals Credenza's HP. Hierophany's Return ★★★★ A Passive that increases the number of uses of the Trait Skill [Hierophany] by 1. It allows you to use the extremely powerful Hierophany consecutively, but it takes a long time to activate unless you equip the Trinket Resonant Crystal, so this Skill is recommended if you have it. Hymn of Absolution ★★★★ A Skill that heals nearby allies and provides buffs to increase their offensive capabilities. If you have Divine Revelation, it also grants Radiant Grace, which further increases offensive capabilities, so equip it when you want to boost your allies' damage. Trial of Radiance ★★★★ A Passive that allows you to gain Divine Revelation each turn by maintaining the Injured state. Use this as a way to gain Divine Revelation if your Star Level Up is low or you don't have the Trinket Resonant Crystal . Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Flagellant ★★★ A Passive that activates upon taking damage. You can also gain stacks from your own HP-consuming skills, so equip this when you need damage reduction or NRG Recovery. Protection of Light ★★ A Reaction that greatly reduces single-target damage taken. The effect is excellent, and you can reduce the time until you can use [Hierophany] by taking damage, but be careful because for Credenza, who consumes HP, you'll want to position her to avoid taking damage from enemies, especially in high-difficulty stages. Blazing Benediction ★ A single-target healing Skill. While it becomes Instant and easy to use if you have Divine Revelation, it has a low priority for team composition because it lacks additional effects compared to Credenza's other skills. Quiet Prayer ★ A Passive that heals nearby allies when on Standby. Be careful when equipping it with [Trial of Radiance] as it also heals Credenza's HP. Battle Flag of Papal State ★ A Leader's Aura with no additional effects. Credenza has many other powerful support skills, so this has a low priority for team composition. Hypnotic Attack ★ The chance to apply Sleep is low, so you should equip the other Basic Attack instead. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Credenza Reorganized Cube Focus Wand Cornucopia Staff We recommend the Reorganized Cube to enhance healing Skills, the Focus Wand to extend the range of single-target skills, and the Cornucopia Staff for additional NRG. Recommended Trinkets for Credenza Resonant Crystal Space-time Ring Fafnir the Scarlet Armor Resonant Crystal is the most recommended option. It shortens the time to use [Hierophany] by consuming HP and allows you to gain [Divine Revelation] through reposition → [Standby]. Other compatible options include the Space-time Ring , which helps with Credenza, who tends to lag behind catch up with allies, or Trinkets that raise stats or provide ally support. Recommended Tarots for Credenza Mercy of The High Priestess Course of Fortune Redemption of The Judgement We recommend options like High Priestess and Empress, which enhance the effectiveness of healing skills; Fortune, which provides various effects such as NRG recovery; and Judicator, which increases the damage enemies take in single-target battles.
