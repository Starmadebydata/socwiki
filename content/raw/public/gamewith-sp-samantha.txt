Follow-up attacks from her Trait on enemies inflicted with [Judgement Brand] and an [Alert] Skill that interrupts enemy actions grant her a very high number of attacks, possessing enough overwhelming offensive power to wipe out an entire group of enemies on her own. Manage Unique Skill Effects --> Management of [Radiant Watch] and [Judgement Brand] is crucial. Understand how to apply them and their effects to significantly boost SP Samantha's offensive capabilities, as well as that of her allies. Skill Effect Mechanics Radiant Watch Details Effect Grants an Aura effect to allies within 5 tiles of self or the entire battlefield at 5-stars . At the end of the turn, removes 1 debuff + grants [Radiant Blessing], and before an ally from the or Factions attacks with a Class Skill, applies [Judgement Brand] for 2 turns. Radiant Blessing: When taking damage, restore HP equal to 30% of M.ATK. Max 2 stacks. Consumes 1 stack upon activation. When possessed, damage dealt by [Judgement Strike] increases from 30%→100%. Ways to Apply At 3-stars or higher, gained at the start of battle until the start of the next turn. Using the Skills [Prayer of Devotion], [Judgement Sanctum], or [Oath to End All Evil] will make it last until the start of the next 2 turns. Judgement Brand Details Effect Damage taken +10%, AoE damage taken an additional +5%. When SP Samantha attacks an enemy with this debuff, if she is 4-stars or lower, Crit Rate +15%, if 5-stars, +50%. After SP Samantha's turn ends, activates [Judgement Strike] on enemies with Judgement Brand, dealing 30% damage if she doesn't have [Radiant Watch], and 100% damage if she does. Ways to Apply Applied before SP Samantha or a character from the or Factions with [Radiant Watch] attacks an enemy. Synergizes with Aggression & Papal States --> By deploying characters from the and Factions at the same time, those characters can apply [Judgement Brand] before they attack. Additionally, the powerful Leader's Aura [Will to Cleanse All Foul] can significantly boost the offensive capabilities of Aggression characters, so be especially mindful of this when forming your party. Can Also Heal and Apply Buffs --> In addition to being an effective attacker, SP Samantha can grant the [Radiant Blessing] buff, which heals when damage is taken, and also applies a buff that nullifies troublesome effects like "Healer's Bane". While it's a lower priority to build SP Samantha for support, she can also apply a powerful M.DEF reduction debuff, making her a versatile character capable of various strategies, not just a pure attacker. Needs Movement and CD Reduction Support While SP Samantha has overwhelming offensive power, her weaknesses are her lack of movement and the long CDs of her main Skills . It's important to support her with characters who can increase Move and provide CD reduction methods, such as Cocoa's [Iron Pot Stew], Taair's skills, or the Tactical Skill "Flag of Convallaria". Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for SP Samantha Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ Initially, you'll want to raise SP Samantha to 3★ to receive [Radiant Watch] at the start of every battle. Eventually, you will want to get her to 5★ to amplify the effects of [Judgement Brand], and grant [Radiant Watch] a global range, as well as an NRG Recovery effect. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Judgement Sanctum ★★★★★ A Skill that can perform a very powerful [Alert] that interrupts enemy actions up to 3 times in one turn. Since its CD is long, be aware that it becomes even more powerful by having a means to reduce its CD. Will to Cleanse All Foul ★★★★★ A Leader's Aura that greatly increases the damage dealt by Aggression Faction characters, including SP Samantha herself. Be aware that it has particularly good synergy with characters who primarily use AoE attacks. Oath to End All Evil ★★★★★ A Skill that attacks enemies in a wide 5-tile radius around the user. Thanks to SP Samantha's Trait, it can also apply [Judgement Brand], making it an extremely powerful Skill that can annihilate enemies when combined with the Trait's follow-up attacks and the [Alert] from [Judgement Sanctum]. Equip this with the highest priority. Protection of Light ★★★★ A Reaction that greatly reduces damage taken from single-target attacks. Equip it when you want to increase durability. Light of Sanction ★★★★ For a Basic Attack, it has a long Range and also deals damage within 1 tile of the target area. Note that it cannot be used on enemies within 1-2 tiles. Indestructible Faith ★★★★ A Reaction that activates [Resolve] by consuming 3 NRG. Although the NRG cost is high, [Resolve] is often useful, especially in high-difficulty stages, so prioritize equipping it. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Prayer of Devotion ★★★ An excellent support Skill that instantly heals and applies "Anti Heal Immunity" and "Bound Resistance" buffs. Equip it when you want to support allies in addition to attacking, or when you want to nullify troublesome debuffs. Scorching Attack ★★★ A Basic Attack that inflicts Scorch on enemies. It has a shorter Range than [Light of Sanction], but its ability to attack enemies within 1-2 tiles is a great advantage. Crystal Implosion - Extra ★★★ A Skill that can deal damage and amplify Magic DMG debuff over a wide area. The debuff is excellent, but since you'll want to specialize SP Samantha in attacking, only equip this when you have many other AoE-focused characters and no one else can apply the debuff. Light of Judgement for All Sins ★★ An easy-to-use AoE attack with a wide forward range and no CD. Among SP Samantha's Skills, it lacks additional effects, so only equip it at low Levels or when you absolutely need a Skill with no CD. NRG Restoration ★★ Equip this only at low Levels when using [Light of Judgement for All Sins] and [Fire Purification] as your main skills, or when you want to utilize the Reaction [Indestructible Faith]. Fire Purification ★★ An AoE Skill with a rare debuff that reduces the enemy's Ranged attack range by 1. Its lack of a CD and its ability to create [Burning] tiles are also excellent, so use it as a main skill in combination with [NRG Restoration] at low Levels. NRG Recovery ★★ A Reaction that provides low-potency damage reduction and an NRG Recovery effect when taking damage. Use it for NRG Recovery at low Levels, or equip it on low-difficulty stages where taking damage is not a problem. Chain of Regeneration ★ Equip this only when you cannot include other support characters and healing is absolutely necessary. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for SP Samantha Light to Purge All Evil Diffusing Prism Nirvana The Signature Weapon Light to Purge All Evil is the most recommended as it can significantly increase Critical Rate & Critical Damage. Other compatible Weapons include Diffusing Prism, which enhances AoE Attacks, and Nirvana, which applies Damage Over Time on a Critical Hit in addition to AoE Damage. Recommended Trinkets for SP Samantha Blood Luxite Amulet Shield of Justice Roaring Flint Trinkets such as the Blood Luxite Amulet, which greatly increases AoE damage, and the Shield of Justice, which raises stats, are recommended. The Roaring Flint is also a compatible Trinket as it helps to apply [Judgement Brand]. Recommended Tarots for SP Samantha Verdict of Justice Dream of The Magician Temptation of The Devil At 5-stars, Justice is the most recommended, since it virtually guarantees a critical hit when combined with the Critical Rate buff and the Weapon Light to Purge All Evil . Other good options include The Magician , which significantly increases AoE damage, Devil , which increases stats, and The Tower , which increases [Alert] and Trait damage. Recommended Engravings for SP Samantha Resonance Effect Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Sword Pentacle When [unharmed], increases ATK and DEF by 12% . Wand Cup At the end of the turn, there is a 50% chance to gain [Engraving Resonance]. Effect: When using an [active skill] next time, refunds the NRG consumed and resets the CD. The skill effect has a CD of 4 turns. (Does not trigger in real-time combat. The effect changes to increasing HP, ATK and DEF by 5%) The [Sword + Wand] and [Sword + Pentacle] combinations, which increase stats, are especially recommended. Other good combinations include [Wand + Cup], which reduces CD & NRG consumption to 0, and [Wand + Wand], which allows you to gain many buffs. SP Samantha's Skin Initial Ascended ▲ Unlock Ascended Skin when character reaches Star Level 5. Samantha rose from the Grand Sanctuary's orphanage through sheer brilliance. The Hierophant personally adopted this exceptional child and provided her with an elite education. She matured into one of his most formidable Judges, trusted with divine authority. Judge Samantha embraced her mentor's teachings completely. She struck down evil without mercy and never hesitated when duty required bloodshed. Yet this brutal path slowly poisoned her spirit. Questions plagued her mind about whether her actions truly served justice. She forced herself to wear the cold mask of Judge and buried her torment deep within. The Sword of Convallaria changed everything. When she fought for peace alongside these noble souls, they awakened her authentic nature. She discovered her true purpose: True love for all, bestowing hope. Titles like Radiant Maidservant and Judge became empty words. The mas no longer hid her heart but revealed it. Samantha then took her first steps toward reforming the Light of Sanctuary.
