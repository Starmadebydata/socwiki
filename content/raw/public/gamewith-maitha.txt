While her playstyle doesn't change significantly, her supporting abilities like Assisting Cover and healing are enhanced, making it easier to utilize attack Skills with long CDs. Excellent Early Support Maitha's strength lies in her ability to take physical attacks for her allies with RK 3 Skill [Come On, Everybody!]. Furthermore, this skill heals allies, as well as buffs them with ATK I and P.DEF I. Healing is a bonus [Come On, Everybody!] is primarily used to grant allies buffs. You will still want a dedicated healer like Innana, Samantha, or Angel included in your lineup for healing. Essential to Increase Defense Due to Maitha's kit, she will often take the brunt of damage instead of your other characters. Therefore, it becomes imperative that you increase your Defense with Rank 1 Skill [Co-Defense], Rank 11 Skill [Invulnerable], and equipment. Increasing Maitha's durability will increase the durability of the entire party. Beware of Breakers and M.ATKs Maitha is strong against physical attacks, but you need to be cautious as she will take severe damage from M.ATK and Breaker enemies. Alternative Knock Back Build In high-level content like the Tower of Conqeust or the Tower of Adversity, Maitha will no longer be an effective tank due to her lack of strong defensive buffs. Instead, you could opt to spend Castallia to unlock R3 [Maitha's Charge] and R11 [Maitha's Combo]. In combination with [Leg Slam], these skills allow Maitha to have three different knock back skills, which lets you push high-level enemies off cliffs or into deep water. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Maitha Aim to Reach 4★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 4★ Maitha isn’t a top priority for upgrades right away, but if youwant to invest in her, aiming for 4-stars is worth considering. At that star level, her ability to heal improves from healing just one ally at the end of her turn to healing two. This small change really adds up in longer battles. So, if you’re actively collecting Memory Shards and thinking long-term, pushing her to 4-stars can make a meaningful difference in your team’s survivability. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Maitha Dawnmoon Newborn Blade Cornucopia Dagger The [Dueling Dagger] is Maitha's best early game build, which is when you will use Maitha the most. Later on, you will have better Defenders. For late-game Maitha, the [Newborn Blade] is recommended, alternatively, since her NRG consumption is high, the [Cornucopia Dagger] is also recommended. Recommended Trinkets for Maitha Mystery Guardian Cooling Powder Old Scrolls of Auguste When focusing on durability, [Mystery Guardian] or [Cooling Powder] are recommended. Otherwise, Trinkets that can support allies by taking advantage of Maitha's high SPD are recommended. Recommended Tarots for Maitha Silence of The Hermit Guidance of the World Temptation of The Devil [The Hermit] for reducing incoming damage, and [The World] for NRG Recovery or Cooldown Reduction are good choices. Recommended Engravings for Maitha Resonance Effect Pentacle Pentacle When [Injured], increases DMG by 5% and decreases the DMG taken by 5% . When [Dying], increases DMG by an additional 10% and decreases the DMG taken by an additional 10% . Wand Pentacle Decreases physical DMG taken by 15% . As a Defender that utilizes [Assisting Block] to defend allies, we recommend [Pentacle + Pentacle] which makes Maitha more durable a she loses HP. [Wand + Pentacle] is also a viable choice, since the majority of the enemies in the game will typically attack using physical damage, and finally, [Wand + Wand], which gradually strengthens Maitha as the battle continues. A supporter and initial member of the Sword of Convallaria. Although orphaned from a young age, Maitha found a home when Rawiyah adopted her and settled in town. Despite her tragic childhood, she never lost her smile and quickly won everyone's love with her kindness and trust in others. In order to protect the people she loves, she volunteered to join the mercenary group established by Rawiyah . Maitha firmly believes that as long as one doesn't lose hope, everything will eventually turn out alright.
