Allies with [Pledge] gain numerous buffs such as increasing and decreasing DMG dealt and taken, HP and NRG recovery every turn, and Assisting Cover from Ayishah. This makes Ayishah flexible since she enables glass cannons to stay alive, or protects backline supporters . Pledge Buff acquired by an ally when [Moon's Caress] is used on them. DMG Dealt +10%, DMG Taken -10% (effect doubles when Ayishah becomes 4-stars). Additional Effects of Pledge Description 1 When Ayishah has the "On Duty" buff, at the end of her turn, she heals herself and the character with "Pledge". Heal amount is 40% of Ayishah's P.ATK value; if she is 5-stars, it's 80%, and also removes 2 debuffs/recovers 1 NRG Pt. 2 When Ayishah uses Skill [Pure Radiance], removes 1 debuff from the character with "Pledge"/heals for 60% of P.ATK value/grants △ATK II. 3 When Ayishah uses Skill [Moonlit Shadow], for 3 turns, she teleports to the tile next to the character with "Pledge", shares 70% of DMG taken, and if Injured, both take -30% DMG. Offeensive and Defensive Powerhouse Since increasing Ayishah's DEF also increases her ATK, the effects of skills like [On Duty], [Pledge], [Dawnmoon], etc. amplify both her offensive and defensive capabilities, making her an excellent tank. By including additional [Instant] skills and Counterattacks, Ayishah can gain many actions, which also makes her a very effective attacker. By nature of her design, Ayishah balances excellent offensive and defensive performances. Highly Mobile Ayishah has a particularly high movement range compared to other Defenders, and even though Ayishah has no skills that increase her movement range, she posses two potent Charge Skills, [Dawnmoon - Moonrise] and [Dawnmoon - Waxing]. Furthermore, since [Dawnmoon - Moonrise] resets CD and returns NRG cost once per turn when an enemy is defeated, by combining it with movement and [Dawnmoon - Waxing], it is possible to move Ayishah a total of 12 tiles in a single turn . Excels in Disruption By slotting [Moonlit Shadow] and [Counterattack], Ayishah becomes an effective disruptor . This combination grants Ayishah the ability to return to the side of the Pledged character, reliably protecting them while intercepting enemies with Counterattacks that can be performed up to 6 times. [Moonlit Shadow] can also be used to protect Seekers with higher movement that often overextend themselves, making it very powerful depending on the stage . Decide Her Role in Advance Ayishah excels in both offense and defence, which grants her a stable, high performance. However, be aware that she cannot obtain the [On Duty] effect if not within 3 tiles of the ally granted [Pledge], and since her Move itself is low, support from allies or tactical skills is needed until engaging enemies. Consider which ally to grant [Pledge], where to move within 3 tiles, build choices, etc., based on the stage and party composition to make Ayishah shine. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Ayishah Aim to Reach 3★ → 4★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 4★ → 5★ Star level ups improve Ayishah’s stats and enhance the effects of her [Pledge] buff. Aim for 3-star first to strengthen [Pledge], then 4-stars to boost her [On Duty] effect. Ultimately, 5-stars is the goal—it grants a major stat increase along with powerful effects like significant HP regeneration and +1 NRG each turn. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Dawnmoon - Moonrise ★★★★★ Instant Charge Skill that inflicts a +20% Damage Taken debuff and disables Basic Attacks and Skills. Damage increases if you’re [On Duty] or facing a single enemy. Defeating an enemy recovers the NRG and removes the cooldown, so try to finish off foes with this. Shape of Full Moon ★★★★★ An excellent Reaction the heals significantly up to 3 times per turn when damaged, and also reduces incoming damage via [Block] and -10% Damage Taken. Generally worth equipping. Dawnmoon ★★★★★ A powerful passive that when [Healthy], increases Damage Dealt by 30% and reduces the cooldown of non-Equipment Class Skills by 1 upon Active Attack. Great for boosting the uptime of [Dawnmoon - Moonrise] and [Moonlit Shadow] Dispelling Strike ★★★★★ Excellent for removing buffs. This Basic Attack is generally preferred over the alternative. Dawnmoon - Waxing ★★★★★ Charge Skill with high damage. When combined with the [On Duty] buff or the [Dawnmoon] passive, total damage becomes exceptional. With 0 CD and a mobility increase, it’s highly valuable every turn. Moonlit Shadow ★★★★ Warps to target, gains defensive and counter-enhancing buffs, and auto-warps to your allied [Pledge] target every turn for 3 turns. Equip to close distances, charging while protecting, or for counter builds. Lunar Shadow ★★★★ Passive that boosts Counterattack range, power, and number of counters. Combine with [Moonlit Shadow] or [Counterattack] Reaction for interception builds. Useful when going for a counter-focused setup. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Counterattack ★★★ [Shape of Full Moon] is generally better due to healing and mitigation, but if you want more counter hits with [Moonlit Shadow], this is the alternative. Swap based on your strategy. Feverish Attack ★★★ Good synergy with [Healthy] effects due to [Life Steal], but Ayishah often heals via her Trait or Reaction, so additional healing might be redundant. Moonlit Hills ★★ A strong Leader Aura that boosts stats and reduces damage taken. However, Ayishah has many priority Skills already. Consider only when running multiple Fortitude units like Kvare or Cocoa . Leg Slam ★ Equip only if Ayishah absolutely needs knockback utility. Pure Radiance ★ Use only if her Trait alone isn’t enough to heal your [Pledge] target and more healing support is required. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Ayishah Dawnmoon Guardian's Will Nocturnal Glow For Ayishah, the top recommendation is [Dawnmoon], which greatly boosts the defense of both herself and one ally, and also grants a buff when healing. Other strong options include [Guardian's Will], which becomes especially powerful when paired with the [Shield of Justice] trinket. [Nocturnal Glow] and [Newborn Blade] are also great picks, as they provide significant overall stat boosts to support both offense and durability. Recommended Trinkets for Ayishah Shield of Justice Crystal Burst Armor Fafnir the Scarlet Armor The [Shield of Justice] is the top recommendation, since it provides a significant all-around stat boost and grants an effect that increases damage dealt by up to 20% when you have a Physical Shield active. Other great options include the [Crystal Burst Armor], which shines in [Strike Back]-focused playstyles, as well as the [Mystery Guardian], [Fancy Hat], and [Cooling Powder]—all of which enhance durability and help nullify debuffs. Recommended Tarots for Ayishah Temptation of The Devil Hierophant's Laws Destruction of The Tower [Devil] is Ayishah's best-in-slot Tarot, since it boosts all stats and works especially well with her Trait that converts a portion of her defense into physical attack. Other strong options include [Heirophant] and [Magician] for increased damage output, [Tower] when focusing on [Strike Back]s, and [Hermit] for damage mitigation. Recommended Engravings for Ayishah Resonance Effect Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Sword Cup When actively attacking, increases the DMG by 5% and grants 15% of [Life Steal]. Pentacle Pentacle When [Injured], increases DMG by 5% and decreases the DMG taken by 5% . When [Dying], increases DMG by an additional 10% and decreases the DMG taken by an additional 10% . Because Ayishah's Trait provides significant stat boosts, [Sword + Wand] is especially recommended. Additionally, since some Skills activate while Ayishah is Healthy, [Sword + Cup]—with its [Life Steal] effect—is also a strong choice. As Inanna's Guard Knight, Ayishah grew up alongside Inanna and received swordsmanship training from the late Queen Sylvia. Ayishah embodies a gentle, approachable demeanor coupled with a calm, decisive nature. These traits allow one to sense a steadfastness in her gentleness. Her decisiveness on the battlefield is driven by a heart that seeks to protect others, rather than cold ruthlessness. To Inanna , she is a loyal and reliable personal Guardian, and more so, a dear friend and sister. After Sylvia's death, Inanna , who saw Sylvia as an ideal role model, was deeply affected. The suspicion between siblings and the occurrence of the Waverun City Incident plunged Inanna into great anguish and self-doubt, but Ayishah always firmly believed in and encouranged Inanna .
