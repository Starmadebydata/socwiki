While its DMG can be negligible, you can boost its potency by increasing the amount of hits or the damage it deals via her main Skills. Furthermore, she can offer utlitiy by applying debuffs such as the rare [Ranged Vulnerable] and [Ward Broken] to enemies afflicted by [Primal Ward]. Burst Charge Management is Essential Anna can stack Burst Charge via her Trait and some of her skills. You should always be mindful of the number of Burst Charge stacks while fighting since it affects Anna's number of attacks or the damage she deals. Consider skills like [Focused Mark] or [Resourceful Tactics] to generate Burst Charge Stacks. Burst Charge Details How to Stack Burst Charges Trait: - Gains 1-2 stacks at the start of action Affected by Star Level - Gains 1-3 stacks at the start of battle Affected by Star Level [Resourceful Tactics]: - Gain 2 stacks when using an Item such as an Interactive/Trigger Object via Auxiliary Passive Twice per turn [Focused Mark]: - At the end of an action, convert all NRG into Burst Charge until NRG is 2 or less, via Auxiliary Passive. Stops at 10 Stacks - Gains 1 stack when using an Item such as an Interactive/Trigger Object Once per turn How to Use Burst Charge [For the Forsaken!]: After use, the number of Alert attacks from the Trait increases by the number of Burst Charge stacks, and 1 Burst Charge is consumed when Alert is triggered. [Hunter's Arsenal]: - [Burst Shot] consumes all Burst Charge after an attack to add 40% Physical DMG for each stack. - [Armor-Piercing Shot] activates the same effect as [Burst Shot] if the target has "Primal Ward or Ward Broken". Requires Slotting [For the Forsaken!] and [Preemptive Intercept]: - Gain additional Preempt charges according to the number of Burst Charge stacks, and consume 1 Burst Charge upon activation. [Focused Mark]: - After using it on a specific enemy and then using [For the Forsaken!], Alert will consume all NRG & Burst Charge to deal an additional 40% Physical DMG for each stack to that enemy. Alerts for Both Single and Multi-Targets [For the Forsaken!] increases the DMG dealt by [Alert] while also adding attacks according to the number of Burst Charges, making it powerful against multiple enemies. [Focused Mark] consumes all Burst Charges to deal additional damage to a single enemy, allowing Anna to perform effectively against single-targets or bosses. Note! [Focused Mark] also has a powerful Auxiliary Passive! So try to avoid using the active skill unless you need to focus attacks on a single target. Weakens Enemies with Rare Debuffs Anna can provide team-wide utility with debuffs such as [Ward Broken], which nullifies any [Primal Ward]s, and the rare [Ranged Vulnerable III] and [▽P.DEF III]. In particular, applying [Ward Broken] allows the entire party to deal effective damage against certain enemies . Note! Anna herself doesn't need to rely on [Ward Broken] since her Trait allows her to bypass [Primal Ward]. Buffs Off-Turn Characters Anna has very powerful Leader's Aura [Unyielding North] , which greatly buffs Characters oriented towards using [Alert], [Strike Back], and [Preempt] . It significantly increases the number of times these effects can trigger, and the damage they deal, compensating for skills that tend to have low damage. May Require Build Swapping Since Anna's kit is so versatile, you may often need to swap out her build for something that fits specific content better. You might need her to focus on single targets, multiple targets, or even support. This also means you have to decide how you want to stack Burst Charges. Plan out her build and moves in advance according to your needs to really make Anna shine . Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Anna Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ Anna steadily gets stronger as she gains higher Star Levels, especially when she can generate more Burst Charge stacks and whenever her Ranged/Alert attacks are improved. Since the number of Burst Charges stacks is directly related to her overall performance, we recommend aiming for 5★ if you intend on using her a lot. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Preemptive Intercept ★★★★★ - Excellent Preempt + Interrupt. - More effective if [For the Forsaken!] is active. Focused Mark ★★★★★ - Focuses on 1 enemy. - Excellent for stacking Burst Charge via Auxiliary Passive. - Can be equipped just for the Auxiliary Passive without ever using the skill. Invigorating Strike ★★★★★ - A Basic Attack that restores NRG. Resourceful Tactics ★★★★ - Lets you choose from 4 skills. - Stacks Burst Charge with its Auxiliary Passive. - Equip when you want more Burst Charges. For the Forsaken! ★★★★ - Damage + Alert buff. - Greatly increases the number of Alerts. - Priority for Alert builds. Hunter's Arsenal ★★★★ - 3 Skills in One. - [Lacerating Shot] grants access to Ward Broken and P.DEF reduction. - [Burst Shot] deals high DMG vs. single targets. Unyielding North ★★★★ - A Leader's Aura that greatly enhances Alerts. - Increases damage against enemies with [Primal Ward] or [Ward Broken]. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Freezing Slash ★★★ - Easy-to-use Instant Movement skill. - Also excellent for gaining buffs. - Equip when you need mobility. Repelling Volley ★★ - Powerful damage and Knockback. - Recommended when you need Ranged Knockback. Heart of the North ★★ - Excellent NRG Recovery effect. - Equip when desperate for NRG. Survival Master ★ - Equip when you want to reduce DMG from hazards (e.g. Burning Tiles, etc.). Powerful Attack ★ - Deals more damage on its own, but the other Basic Attack generates more NRG which leads to more Burst Charges. Tundra Raider ★ - Immunity to Frosty Tiles is already provided by Trait.
