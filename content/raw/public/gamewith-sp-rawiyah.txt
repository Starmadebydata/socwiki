However, due to her low durability, she requires support from allies through abilities like Assisting Cover and careful decision-making on whether to advance or retreat. Can Act Again With Trait SP Rawiyah can act again after performing an area attack due to her Trait. While she enters a "Fatigue" state afterward, preventing further actions for a set number of turns, reaching 5 Stars removes this limitation, enabling her to act again every turn. Unleashes Full Potential at 5 Stars Once upgraded to 5 Stars, SP Rawiyah becomes capable of acting again every turn, significantly enhancing her single-target attack performance. With her basic attack "Powerful Attack" and "Gale - Void Slash", she can deal a total of 250% damage (110% → 15% → 110% → 15%). If you’re looking for a powerful attacker, prioritize collecting Memory Shards to upgrade her. She Gets Stronger With Repeated Attacks By dealing damage to enemies with her skills, she can stack "Stamina", which gradually boosts her Crit, Crit DMG, and DMG. Make use of her ability to Act Again and focus on hitting multiple enemies with AoE attacks to quickly build up her Stamina stacks. Focus on Enhancing Gale Sword - Chaotic Flurry's Damage The wide-area attack "Gale Sword - Chaotic Flurry" increases in power when you’ve attacked enemies beforehand. It can deal up to a total of 150% damage, so make sure to use Trinket skill, Instant skills, or attacks during the Act Again phase before unleashing Gale Sword - Chaotic Flurry for maximum effectiveness. Possesses the SP Skill If you own both SP Rawiyah and the original Rawiyah, you can unlock an additional SP Skill for each character by meeting specific conditions. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for SP Rawiyah Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ Star level ups reduce the duration of SP Rawiyah's [Fatigue] state. Aim to reach 3-stars first, where [Fatigue] is reduced to 2 turns, and make 5-star your top priority—at that point, she can act again every turn. For high-level content, losing units leads to a swift Game Over , so we recommend using this reaction skill only when partnered with allies who use summons, since losing summons triggers her reaction without counting towards defeated units. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Gale - Piercing Clouds ★★★★★ An excellent skill with numerous benefits such as triggering the Trait's [Act Again], as a means of movement, activating [Gale - Void Slash], and increasing damage dealt by [Gale Sword - Chaotic Flurry]. Useful in all situations and should always be equipped. Gale - Void Slash ★★★★★ A skill that triggers an additional AoE attack after attacking, dealing minor damage + applying a [Lv2 Attribute Debuff]. If equipped, it activates even after a single-target attacks and can trigger the Trait's [Act Again] effect. Note that it does not trigger from using Trinket Skills. I'm! Mad! Now! ★★★★★ A Reaction that grants [Resolve] and pairs well with SP Rawiyah, who is a Seeker with a low HP pool. The effect 'When an ally is defeated' also activates when a Summoned Unit is defeated, making it excellent for easily gaining buffs through combinations with other characters. Try The Punch! ★★★★★ A Basic Attack that deals low damage + 1-tile knockback then transitions into another attack with high damage + 2-tile knockback. Because it makes it easier to aim for collisions or fall kills, this Basic Attack is generally recommended over the other. Gale Dance - Sincerity ★★★★ Although the damage per hit is lower than [Gale Sword - Chaotic Flurry], its ability to perform consecutive AoE attacks and its low cost are nice. Since it's a self-centered AoE, it's easy to land, but be aware that [Gale Dance - Resolution] has a narrower range. Gale Sword - Chaotic Flurry ★★★★ A Skill that deals damage in a wide area in front. If you cast any skills twice before use, it will deal more damage even against single targets, but be careful as activating it directly results in low damage for its NRG cost. Powerful when considering height and rage even in single-target battles. Powerful Attack ★★★★ Since it deals high damage, this Basic Attack is recommended against enemies immune to knockback. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Another Pint (SP Skill) ★★★ A Skill that grants Stamina, Armor Piercing, and Crit Rate Up. In general, it's better to focus on Skills that help activate [Gale - Void Slash], so consider equipping this only when you can’t apply defense debuffs, need quick Stamina in short battles, or are focusing on single-target fights. Mobile Crew ★★ Easy to activate, but generally not recommended as there are many other Skills you'll want to prioritize. Equip only if it's essential to ignore enemy Reactions. Gale - Draw Blade ★★ Provides NRG recovery and has decent synergy, but it's usually better to rely on other characters for energy support. Only equip this Skill if you don’t have access to external energy support and absolutely need self-supplied NRG. Fancy Footwork ★★ Worth considered in stages where enemies can one-hit KO your characters. Otherwise, you usually want to orient SP Rawiyah towards offense. Hunting Impulse ★★ SP Rawiyah's only single-target attack Skill. Its NRG cost is a bit high, but with no cooldown, it can be used every turn as long as you have a way to re-supply NRG. Vital Guard ★ Since triggering [Dodge] requires entering the [Dying] state, it's a fairly risky Reaction. Generally [I'm! Mad! Now!] is the preferable Reaction. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for SP Rawiyah Gale Sword Feast Axe Tuning Hammer The [Gale Sword], which increases physical attack and critical damage after you [Act Again], is especially recommended for SP Rawiyah. Other effective options depending on the stage include the [Feast Axe], which pairs well with [Gale – Piercing Clouds], the [Tuning Hammer] for buff removal, and the [Denial Hammer] to strengthen knockback effects. Recommended Trinkets for SP Rawiyah Shield of Justice Roaring Flint Maverick's Cloak For SP Rawiyah, the [Shield of Justice] is the top recommendation—it boosts her stats and grants a Physical Shield, which fits perfectly with her front-line role. Other great options include trinkets with offensive skills that help build up her [Stamina”]Trait, the [Maverick’s Cloak], which suits her aggressive playstyle, and the [Evergreen Pendant], which can stack multiple buffs to further enhance her performance. Recommended Tarots for SP Rawiyah Verdict of Justice Dream of The Magician Temptation of The Devil [Justice] compliment's SP Rawiyah's damage output due to its critical enhancement effects. Other strong options include [Magician], which greatly boosts AoE damage, and [Devil], which provides a broad stat increase. SP Rawiyah's Skin Initial Ascended ▲ Unlock Ascended Skin when character reaches Star Level 5. The founder and leader of the Sword of Convallaria who acts as the protector of the eponymous town with her mercenary group. Rawiyah is kind and straightforward, affecting those around her by her sense of justice and warm heart. When facing a crisis, she never backs down from her duty to protect those in need. As a resident, she is always concerned about the situation in Iria. Her skills and insights, which far surpass those of ordinary mercenaries, seem to suggest that she has an unusual past. It was the spring of year 992 of the Radiant Calendar, and recognized as a battle prodigy , Rawiyah graduated from Waverun City King's Army Academy with top honors. With a pure and clear heart, she, like a little sun always full of energy, radiating passion and warmth, was the only one who reached out to help Safiyyah , who was bullied at the academy. This experience makes them inseparable best friends and perfect partners. Despite being fed up with restrictive rules, Rawiyah joined the Mobile Squad of the Security Bureau with Safiyyah after graduation, not only because they vowed to protect Waverun City toghether, but also because she believed that, despite the storms ahead, they would reach their destination — as long as they were together.
