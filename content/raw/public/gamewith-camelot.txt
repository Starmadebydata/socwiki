Additionally, Camelot can leverage his HP and convert a portion of it into P.ATK, as well as effects like [Act Again] make Camelot a formidable character who excels in both offense and defense . Single-turn, 100% Shield Through Camelot's Trait, as well as his Trait Skill [Declaration of Conquest], and finally his [Throne of Ashes] skill, it's possible to generate a Physical Shield worth 100% of his HP in a single turn. Conditional Act Again Camelot's Trait grants him the ability to Act Again when using an AoE attack on an enemy inflicted with the [Coercion] debuff. Additionally, with his Reaction [King's Fighting Spirit], he can gain "Act Again or Extra Action" when attacked by an enemy, meaning he has two sources for [Act Again], giving him a very high number of actions. Unique Buffs and Debuffs --> Camelot has various unique buffs/debuffs, such as the [Coercion] debuff that weakens enemies, the [Regroup] debuff that prevents Camelot from being able to [Act Again], and the Trait Skill [Declaration of Conquest] which grants the [Disdain] buff. Understanding these effects is the key to using Camelot effectively. Buff and Debuff Effect Details Coercion Details Effect Stacks up to 6 times. When attacking Camelot, ATK is decreased. When attacked by Camelot, DEF is decreased. The reduction amount is 1% of Camelot's Max HP. Half (0.5%) of the same effect applies to characters from the Faction. Camelot gains [Act Again] by using an AoE attack on an enemy with [Coercion]. The skill multiplier of [Verdict - World Conquest] and [Crushing] is fixed against enemies with 6 stacks. Application Applies 1-3 stacks before an active AoE attack (changes with Star Level Up). Applies 2 stacks before attacking with [Throne of Ashes]. Applies 6 stacks with Trait Skill [Declaration of Conquest]. Regroup Details Effect An undispellable, self-inflicted debuff applied to Camelot when he gains [Act Again] from his Trait. While possessing Regroup, he cannot [Act Again] from his Trait . Duration: 4 turns at 1-2 stars, 3 turns at 3-4 stars, 2 turns at 5-stars. [Declaration of Conquest] Details Effect An [Instant] Skill with a 5 tile range and CD of 4. Deals 30% AoE damage to one enemy, applies 6 stacks of the [Coercion] debuff for 2 turns, and grants the user the [Disdain] buff for 4 turns and a Physical Shield worth 30% of HP. Disdain effect : Damage to enemies with Coercion +10%, increased to 30% if they have 6 stacks. When attacked by an enemy with 6 stacks of Coercion, if in an [Injured] state, recover 35% HP (Once per turn). High Damage Intervals Camelot is an effective attacker, but his weakness is that there are large gaps between his damage windows . Between Camelot's lack of ways to increase his own Move and the long CD of his main skills and Act Again, Camelot must rely on allied supports while being mindful of Move/CD reduction . Use [Throne of Ashes] to gain a large amount of Physical Shield and attack while taking hits from groups of enemies. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details King's Fighting Spirit ★★★★★ A Reaction that reduces Physical DMG taken and grants SPD Up & Extra Action or Act Again upon being attacked. Although it has a long CD of 5 turns, it allows for many attack opportunities when combined with its Trait's Act Again effect, so prioritize including it in your build. Verdict - Solo Carry ★★★★★ A Skill that changes with each use: "Instant Move & AoE Attack" → Wide-Range Attack → High-Damage Move & AoE Attack Skill. If you can get the Act Again effect from Camelot's Trait, you can use the whole combo in one turn, making it a main skill that deals overwhelming total damage. Crushing ★★★★★ A powerful Basic Attack that deals damage in a wide area and Knocks back enemies in the front row. You should generally equip this Basic Attack. Throne of Ashes ★★★★ A powerful Skill that damages enemies in a wide area while also having the effects of Steal Buffs, Apply 2 stacks of [Coercion], and gains a Physical Shield equal to 10% of Max HP for each enemy hit. Although the damage is low, it can greatly strengthen Camelot, so prioritize equipping it in battles against multiple enemies. Scorn the Masses ★★★★ A Passive that increases HP and restores HP every turn. When attacked by an enemy with lower HP than Camelot, it nullifies some debuffs, Knockback, and Shield Break effects, so prioritize equipping it on stages with enemies that have such effects. Proud Lion Heart ★★★★ A Leader's Aura that greatly strengthens characters of The Union Faction. It also has good synergy with Camelot, who can gain many Physical Shields, so prioritize equipping it if you want to increase attack power. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Strength Activation - Extra ★★★ A Reaction that increases healing effects and grants [△DMG III]. On stages where you don't take damage from enemies, you can't activate the effect of the other Reaction [King's Fighting Spirit], so equip this one instead. Unstoppable Force ★★ Camelot has low Move, so this is a Skill you'll want to equip when you need to supplement his Move or on stages with troublesome enemies that have [Resolve]. Blazing Scourge ★★ A Skill that damages a wide area and applies [✗Healing Received] and [▽P.DEF II]. In high-difficulty stages, some stages have troublesome enemy healing, so equip this on those stages. Will to Survive - Extra ★ A Passive that increases HP, which also raises attack performance at the same time. You can gain a healing effect when [Dying], but [Scorn the Masses], which also increases HP, is easier to use, so you should generally equip that instead. Shield ★ There are many other ways to obtain a Physical Shield, so this has a low priority. Invigorating Strike ★ A Basic Attack with poor synergy, as Camelot generally does not need NRG Recovery. Knight's Glory - Extra ★ Only equip this when you want to grant a Physical Shield to allies. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Camelot Surtr the Darkfire Axe Feast Axe Hollow Axe Camelot's signature Gear, Surtr the Darkfire Axe , is the most recommended as it significantly increases HP and also boosts attack performance when a Physical Shield is gained. If you don't have it, Weapons that increase HP or P.ATK are great alternatives. Recommended Trinkets for Camelot Shield of Justice Roaring Flint Maverick's Cloak We especially recommend Shield of Justice for its Physical Shield & stat bonuses. Another good Trinket is Roaring Flint, which helps apply [Coercion] and activate the Trait's Act Again effect. Recommended Tarots for Camelot The Fool's Naivety Dream of The Magician Temptation of The Devil The Fool works especially well since it largely increases HP, which also directly enhances Camelot's offensive power. Another great Tarot is the Magician, which unconditionally boosts Camelot's damage. The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Wand Wand After using a [basic attack], [skill], [strike back], or [assisting attack], gains 1 random [Level 2 Attribute Buff]. Wand Cup At the end of the turn, there is a 50% chance to gain [Engraving Resonance]. Effect: When using an [active skill] next time, refunds the NRG consumed and resets the CD. The skill effect has a CD of 4 turns. (Does not trigger in real-time combat. The effect changes to increasing HP, ATK and DEF by 5%) Sword + Wand for the stat increases, or Wand + Wand for many buffs. If you find your Camelot lacking HP, you could also run Pentacle + Pentacle since they have the highest HP stat rolls. Camelot, one of the Three Great Knight Kings, rose from the ashes of the Second Radiant War to unite the faltering Knight States into a formidable force, shaping the fate of the entire continent of Rodinia. Time and again, he charged into battle himself, crushing any who dared oppose him. Bold, fiery, and inspiring, Camelot won glory for the Union—and the unwavering loyalty of the knights who rode beside him. Camelot's ideal is to conquer all lands within sight and realize the grand unification of Rodinia, yet he disdains the use of intrigue to achieve this end—under the Predator's Law, might alone is the world's true rule, and so it is by absolute strength that this conquest, belonging to Camelot alone, shall be fulfilled.
