Garbelius June 4, 2025 Table of Contents Overview Kvare - Finance Minister of Iria Roster Skill Recommendations Silencing Attack Sanctuary Emblem This is the Moment for Rain Wither Will of Victory Equipment Additional Notes Victory Before Battle Conviction Overview Kvare - Finance Minister of Iria Overview Kvare is a formidable DPS character who is capable of causing both widespread damage and focused, single-target damage. He can be used in a wide variety of situations, and even possesses skills that can act to support his allies. His entire kit is built around debuffs and building upon them to cause as much damage and chaos as possible to the enemy. Roster Trait Kvare’s trait, [Roster], forms at least part of the backbone of his kit. In addition to boosting his M.ATK and HP stats (by 3-15%), it also gives him an active, [Instant] skill (also called [Roster]), which inflicts a debuff called… [Roster] (At the start of the turn, inflicts 2 random [Level 2 Debuffs] on the target for 2 turns. When [Roster] ends or the target is attacked and enters the [Dying] state, Kvare immediately summons assassins to attack the character, dealing 100% of Kvare's M.ATK as [piercing DMG], then removes [Roster]. This effect cannot be dispelled or immunized.), as well as 30-60% [Life Loss] on a chosen target within 5 tiles. Just be aware that this skill has a cost of 2 NRG, though once Kvare reaches 3-star, he gets 1 NRG refunded the first time he uses the skill each turn (he can use it a second time per turn at 5-star). His trait also boosts his ATK and DEF by 5% each time the [Roster] debuff is removed from an enemy, up to 20%, until the end of the battle (which cannot be immunized or dispelled) and, at the end of his turn, Kvare passively casts the [Roster] skill on the closest enemy within 5 tiles of himself. Skill Recommendations Silencing Attack Basic Attack Of the two options, [Silencing Attack] is the more versatile. Although the target needs to already be [Injured] in order to inflict its secondary [⦸ Active Skills] effect on them, that effect is very powerful, and has the potential to have much more impact than a single stack of [Infection]. Kvare’s piercing damage from his other skills is much better than what infection offers, so you’re better off having this very useful debuff on hand, should it be needed. Sanctuary Emblem Reaction Skill Neither of Kvare’s reaction skills are, in my opinion, overly impressive. Quite frankly, Kvare really would prefer not to be attacked at all, so any skill that is built around him being hit seems a bit counterintuitive, but since one must be chosen, I would say that [Sanctuary Emblem] is more likely to be useful. If you’re keeping Kvare behind your lines, using his skills at long range, then he is more likely to be hit by a large AoE attack than he is a single-target attack. That being said, it might be worth the Castalia to pick up [Entrapping] for content where AoEs aren’t an issue. Honestly, how you go about dealing with his reaction skill is largely up to your own taste, but if you grab both, he does get an extra 3% ATK, so it’s certainly not a waste of a Castalia. This is the Moment for Rain Skill 1 For Kvare’s first skill, you’ll definitely want to bring his ultimate skill, [This is the Moment for Rain] (what an awful skill name). By default, the skill hits every enemy within 6 tiles of Kvare with piercing dmg equal to 40% of Kvare’s M.ATK, and also immediately triggers and removes any [DoT] effects on those enemies. If the target(s) have been inflicted with [Roster], they will also be inflicted with [Piercing Vulnerable II], [⦸ Reaction Skill], [⦸ Passive Skill] for 2 turns, and [Roster] is also triggered and removed from those targets. That means that this attack is best used on multiple enemies who have already been hit with [Roster], and (if you don’t pick his [Wither] passive) enemies who have multiple [DoT] effects on them. This skill has no NRG cost, but it has a relatively prohibitive CD of 3 turns. Since this skill constitutes much of Kvare’s offensive capability, he benefits from effects that reduce CD, like [Happiness] or [Flag of Convallaria]. Wither Skill 2 While [This is the Moment for Rain] is a solid skill, it can be made even better by bringing Kvare’s passive skill, [Wither]. This passive modifies his ultimate so that it no longer triggers and/or removes [DoT] effects, but instead doubles the damage that the skill does. Additionally, when Kvare actively deals damage with any non-[Instant] skill, he additionally inflicts 60% of his M.ATK as [Life Loss] on the target(s) for 1 turn. This skill effectively swaps out attempting to trigger other [DoTs] (which are often resisted by endgame enemies) with both raw damage and his M.ATK-based piercing DMG, which is much more reliable, since it isn’t resisted by enemies who resist percent-based piercing DMG. The second part of this passive’s effect also applies to any other attack skills that Kvare uses, meaning it has more uses than just his ultimate. Will of Victory Skill 3 Kvare’s final skill slot is a bit of a flex slot, but (at least for shorter content), [Will of Victory] is usually the best option. This skill is actually several skills-in-one, all of which can only be used once per battle, in the following specific order: (1) [Will of Victory]: Instant. Grants [Potential Burst] to the target. (2) [Flaming Winds]: Deals 60% [fire] [AoE DMG] to enemies within range and inflicts 2 stacks of [Ignition]. (3) [Fortune of All]: Instant. Recovers all NRG of the target. (4) [Chill of Ocean]: Deals 100% [ice] [AoE DMG] to enemies within range and inflicts [Frostbite]. (5) [Golden Power]: Instant. Recovers all HP of the target. (6) [Wrath of Thunder]: Single-target attack. Deals 180% DMG, dispels all [buffs] on the target before attacking, and immediately triggers [Roster] and all [DoT] effects on the target after attacking. This skill’s biggest downside is that it becomes an empty slot after using all six skills. As such, be careful how you use the skills, and make the most of them. The biggest advantage of this skill is the fact that it uses no NRG, which means he can save his NRG for [Roster]. Equipment For a weapon, Kvare really wants his Astral Imprint, Nightmare Puppet, as it synergizes perfectly with his kit. It buffs his M.ATK, off of which his piercing DMG scales, and allows his attacks to inflict an extra debuff, as well as the unique [Nightmare Puppet] debuff, which increases piercing DMG received by 5-15% (depending on the star level of the weapon). Since almost all of Kvare’s damage is piercing, this translates to a noticeable increase in his overall damage output. If you do not have access to his Astral Imprint, however, Diffusing Prism also synergizes with his kit beautifully. For a trinket, you can either opt for Shield of Justice or Origin Hourglass; the former provides a bigger, but conditional boost to his M.ATK (though if he never gets hit, this isn’t an issue), whereas the latter provides a smaller, unconditional one, so it’s largely a matter of taste. Maverick’s Cloak can also be worth considering if he’s going to be moving away from allies before using his attacks. For a tarot, truly the only one you should be considering is Devil, since it directly increases his M.ATK stat, which is all he cares about. Fortune can be useful for NRG and all the other usual Fortune benefits, but in most cases will be outperformed by Devil. World can be considered if you’re interested in getting his ultimate back up as quickly as possible. For engravings, I suggest Sword + Wand, since it boosts his M.ATK. Sword + Pentacle is okay, since he doesn’t like to take damage anyway, but the ATK ceiling on Pentacle engravings is lower than Wand engravings, so you end up losing out on M.ATK that way. When rolling for stats, you should prioritize M.ATK. Additional Notes Flavia has a number of other useful skills that need to be discussed here: Victory Before Battle Alt. Skill 1 [Victory Before Battle] is worth considering instead of [Conviction] if your primary goal is to simply spam Kvare’s ultimate and not use any other skills besides it and [Roster]. This skill is a passive which causes Kvare to heal 20% of his HP and gain 1 NRG, plus 2 [Level 2 Buffs] every time an enemy with [Roster] is defeated. This can help keep Kvare topped up on NRG so he can use [Roster] each turn, but if you’re going to run this, make sure you have a way to reduce the CD of his ultimate, or you’re going to have a plethora of dead turns and Kvare is going to feel very slow. Conviction Alt. Skill 2 [Conviction] (his default skill) has a range of 4 tiles and inflicts [Life Loss] on the target equal to 100% of Kvare’s M.ATK, as well as inflicts them with [▼ Move I] and [▼ SPD II] for 2 turns. Bear in mind that this does not immediately inflict any damage. However, if the target has [Roster], then this skill also removes all of its [buffs] and (while it shortens the [Life Loss] duration by 1 turn) deals piercing DMG equal to 100% of Kvare’s M.ATK to the target. This is actually quite a powerful attack, and (as long as you don’t need the enemy dead immediately), it’s even effective without [Roster] on the target. It can be used every turn and costs only 2 NRG, meaning that it can be combined well with [Roster] on turns where you can’t use his ultimate, or in more single-target scenarios.
