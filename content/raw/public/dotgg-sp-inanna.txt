Due to shifts in meta, she has gone from being used primarily as a DPS to being used primarily in a supporting role, so this guide has been updated to reflect that Perhaps the most noteworthy part of her kit is her ability to provide allies with bonus actions with the use of an [Instant] skill that is cooked into her trait—a skill which also gives herself a bonus action when she reaches 5-star! SP Inanna is sure to take your account to new heights! Free Will Trait SP Inanna’s trait, [Free Will], largely functions around her having stacks of her unique buff, [Free Will]. [Free Will] can stack up to 12 times (she gains 2-3 stacks passively at the end her turn), but when she has at least 8 stacks, she can use an [Instant] skill that comes with her trait, called [Call of Freedom]. This skill costs 2 NRG and consumes 8 stacks of [Free Will], and it heals the target ally (with 5 tiles) for 30-90% of her P.ATK and grants them [Freedom Stance I-II], which allows the target ally to take another action after their next non-[Instant] skill. The only difference between [Freedom Stance I] and [Freedom Stance II] (which she gets at 4-star) is that [Freedom Stance II] also provides 2 NRG to the target ally when they start their bonus action. At 3-star, she also provides the target ally with [▲ DMG II] and [DMG Reduction II] for 2 turns and, at 5-star, she also provides ALL of the above effects to herself! As such, getting her to 5-star is of the utmost importance, as she can very easily take advantage of the bonus actions, since her kit is so versatile. Whenever she has at least one stack of [Free Will], she gets 1-2 extra tiles of movement. She starts the battle with 6-8 stacks of [Free Will] and, at the end of her action, she gains 2-3 more stacks. Lastly, her trait increases her ATK and DEF by 0-25%. Skill Recommendations Sword - Dawnlight Basic Attack Both of SP Inanna’s basic attacks are very good, but [Sword - Dawnlight] is the clearly superior option. The attack has a range of 2-3 tiles, and deals 100% magical AoE DMG to the target. The ability to attack at range is very useful, and the fact that it counts as magical AoE DMG allows the attack to be boosted by her Astral Imprint and/or Blood Luxite Amulet. The alternate option, [Sword - Dusk] is worth mentioning, as it does allow her to do a 1-tile knockback (this also procs one of her passives), but overall, the other skill option will be more beneficial. Battle-Hardened Warrior (or Natural Sword Intent) Reaction Skill Once she reaches 3-star (as long as you also have the base Inanna character) SP Inanna gains access to her SP skill, [Battle-Hardened Warrior]. This skill allows SP Inanna to, whenever ANY character (friend or foe alike) uses a non-[Instant] skill within 5 tiles of SP Inanna, she gains one stack of [Battlefield Experience] (Buff. Increases ATK by 2% and reduces DMG taken by 2%. Can stack up to 15 times. Cannot be dispelled or immunized.) Not only does this provide her with a lot of ATK and DMG reduction, but when she reaches 15 stacks, she also gains [Attack Command] (Aura. Increases the ATK of other friendly units within 2 tiles by 12%.) for the rest of the battle. Additionally, whenever she uses a non-[Instant] skill, she gains a stack of [Free Will]. If you do not have access to this skill, though, the clear choice of reaction skill is [Natural Sword Intent], which gives her [Preempt] before being hit with an active single target attack, once per round. If she has 6 or more stacks of [Free Will] and is defeated, she revives at 35% HP, consumes 6 stacks, and gains [DMG Reduction IX] for 1 turn, but only once per battle. This is a nice skill, but she really is not a character who you want to have getting attacked, and nor do you want her [Free Will] stacks going to anything besides her [Call of Freedom] skill. Without the SP skill, however, that is her best option. Convallaria Swordplay Skill 1 [Convallaria Swordplay] is actually a two-in-one choice skill. The first skill, [Convallaria - Blooming Horizon], deals magical AoE DMG equal to 100% P.ATK to all enemies in a 3x3 grid in front of Inanna, and then grants her [DMG Reduction II]. If it only hits one target, the multiplier increases to 150%, meaning it has good nuking potential, even against bosses. The second skill, [Convallaria - Windswept Blossom], attacks the 3 tiles horizontally in front of her for 150% physical AoE DMG, and knocks the targets back by one tile, and then she gains [DMG Reduction II]. Both skills cost only 2 NRG and have no CD, so if she gets a bonus action (at 5-star), she is guaranteed to be able to use either one! It is also nice that one skill does magical DMG while the other does physical DMG, meaning she has the ability to pass shields on enemies, if needed. Battle Flag of Convallaria Stands Skill 2 For her second skill, you will undeniably want to bring [Battle Flag of Convallaria Stands]. This is an [Instant] skill that summons a [Battle Flag of Convallaria] (Item. A waving battle flag that inspires nearby allies; for all allies from "Iria" or "Sword of Convallaria": Increases ATK by 10% and DEF by 20%; for each 1 ally from "Iria" or "Sword of Convallaria" in battle, increases DMG dealt by 3% and reduces DMG taken by 3%, up to 15%. The flag will disappear after a certain time.) on an empty tile within 3 tiles of Inanna. In addition to the excellent buffs it provides, she then also grants [Co-Attack] and [Co-Defense] to all allies within 5 tiles for 2 turns. After she casts this skill, it changes to [For This World of Peace]. This skill allows her to target a tile within 3 tiles of herself, and unleashes a magical attack equal to 130% P.ATK on all enemies within 2 tiles of the target. Before attacking, she cleanses 5 buffs from each target, and, when only attacking one enemy, the multiplier increases to a staggering 180%! The first skill has a NRG cost and CD of 3, so it definitely cannot be spammed, but both skills are so incredible, they should almost always come with her into battle. Light of Convallaria Skill 3 [Light of Convallaria] provides Inanna with 2 stacks of [Free Will] and also gives all allies within 3 tiles [▲ ATK II] and [▲ Move I] for 2 turns, and gives them all 1 NRG. This is very useful for enabling your entire team to move farther and cause a great deal more damage. It costs 2 NRG and has a CD of 2 turns, which means it can be used every other turn, which is not prohibitive. Equipment For a weapon, Nocturnal Glow is the best option, given that it provides a unique DMG buff to any allies her skills affect. If, however, you are building her for damage (though this is not the most efficient way to use her anymore), Dawnlight will be the best option, followed by Void Stab, and then Newborn Blade. For a trinket, Old Scrolls of Auguste is incredible on her, not only for its SPD manipulation, but its synergy with Nocturnal Glow. Again, if you’re building her for damage, then Blood Luxite Amulet and/or Shield of Justice will be your best options, followed by Origin Hourglass. For a tarot, I still prefer Magician even on her support build, since she still does attack on turns when she is not using [Light of Convallaria], and none of the other typical support-based tarots really shine on her that well. For engravings, I do still suggest Sword + Wand, for similar reasons, but also because Cup + Wand is unreliable on her, since [Call of Freedom] is very likely to use up its resonance with no actual benefit. When rolling for stats, aim for P.ATK, with a secondary focus on HP.
