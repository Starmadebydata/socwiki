However you choose to build her, she will be a powerful addition to your team! The build suggested below is a mixed build that will allow her to be more of a generalist, but I will also cover more specialized Support and DPS builds in the Additional Notes section. Also of note is her rather impressive SPD of 245, meaning she will usually be one of the first characters to go on any given turn, which lets her support her team well. Yennefer of Vengerberg Trait Yen’s trait, [Yennefer of Vengerberg], gives her a 5-15% boost to her M.ATK, which further increases by an additional 10-30% when she has her [Magic Boost] status (“Some skills are replaced with [Magic Empowerment] effects. [Magic Empowerment] skills have no NRG cost and no CD; after use, all skills revert to original skills.”). This status is given to her when she uses the [Instant] skill provided automatically by her trait, [Magic Surge], which very simply gives her [Magic Boost]. She can only use this skill when she does not currently have [Magic Exhaustion] (“Cannot use skill [Magic Surge]; after casting non-[instant] skills or when on [Standby], removes 1 stack.”), which she starts off with 1 stack of pre-3-star. She gains 2 stacks of [Magic Exhaustion] whenever she uses a [Magic Empowerment] skill (this becomes only 1 stack at 5-star). Starting at 4-star, after she uses [Magic Surge], the next non-[Magic Empowerment] skill she uses costs 1 less NRG and has its CD reduced by 1 turn. All of this essentially means that she uses [Magic Surge] when she can, then uses buffed, free versions of her skills, and then uses her normal skills on the turns in between when she is waiting for her stacks of [Magic Exhaustion] to deplete from normal skill use. At the time I am writing this guide, there is currently a bug in game that causes her to lose 2 stacks (instead of 1 stack) of [Magic Exhaustion] pre-5-star when using either [Embrace of Shadows] or [Charming Illusion]. Skill Recommendations Dispelling Strike Basic Attack Both of Yen’s basic attacks have their uses, but I feel that, overall, [Dispelling Strike] is the better option. It will be a rare occasion where she has to resort to using her basic attack at all, but the buff removal effect is more hypothetically useful than the DMG boosting effect of [Charged Attack]. Overall, not super important, and if you are aiming to build her as a DPS, you may actually want to go with [Charged Attack]; there is no wrong choice, here. Evasive Shift Reaction Skill [Evasive Shift] is Yen’s unique reaction, which reduces all DMG taken by 10%, and decreases magical DMG taken by an additional 30%. On top of this, this skill also allows her to, when taking an active single-target attack from the front or side, perform a [Preempt], interrupting the enemy’s attack entirely, as well as deal 50% magical DMG and inflict 2 random [Level 2 Debuffs] on the would-be attacker, at which point she teleports to the closest ally within 2-6 tiles. This second effect has a 2 turn CD. While Yennefer’s skills are rather long-ranged, and she should not often find herself under attack, this reaction provides her with a solid backup plan, should an enemy get too close. Portal Skill 1 [Portal] is a very unique skill, which allows Yen to target any non-immune ally or enemy within 4 tiles of herself, then teleport them to any empty tile within 6 tiles of where they started. When this is used on an ally, it gives them [▲ DMG III] and [▲ SPD II] for 2 turns, while enemies targeted by this effect will instead be dealt 100% magical DMG and be inflicted with [Stunned] (which is a [Bound] status, for those using Witcher characters) for 1 turn. Whether it is an ally or enemy teleported, Yen will then do a second attack on all enemies within 2 tiles of where the target is teleported to for 50% DMG, and inflicts them with [Vulnerable III]. In other words, she can give an ally [▲ DMG III] and inflict [Vulnerable III] on multiple targets in one skill, while also tossing that ally into the midst of those foes, and allowing them to go much sooner. When having [Magic Boost], this skill becomes [Instant], because it clearly was not already broken enough! It’s normal version costs 1 NRG and has a CD of 4 turns, but it will be rare that you actually have to wait that full time to use it again. Embrace of Shadows Skill 2 [Embrace of Shadows] is an AoE attack that Yen can target on any tile within 3 tiles of herself, which hits all targets within 2 tiles for 40% DMG, and inflicts them with 1 random [Level 2 Debuff] for 2 turns. Any allies within this area get healed by 60% of Yen’s M.ATK and are granted [▲ DMG III] and [Frosty and Burning Tile Immunity] for 2 turns. When Yen has [Magic Boost], this skill increases in scope by quite a bit, becoming a 5-tile AoE centered on Yen, which deals 60% DMG, and inflicts 2 random [Level 2 Debuffs] and [▼ DMG II] on all enemy targets hit for 2 turns. Any allies in this area instead get healed by 60% of Yen’s M.ATK (same as the basic version), have 2 debuffs dispelled, and are granted 2 [Level 2 Buffs], [▲ DMG III], and [Frosty and Burning Tile Immunity] for 2 turns. The base version of this skill costs 3 NRG and has no CD, meaning it can be used on any turn where Yen is not using [Portal] (or whatever other skill) or an [Magic Empowerment] skill, giving her team easy access to a powerful AoE DMG buff. Tissaia's Teachings Skill 3 [Tissaia’s Teachings] is a passive which lets Yen regain 1 NRG and reduces all of her class skill CDs by 1 turn whenever she uses a non-[Magic Empowerment] skill, up to 1 time per turn. This is an important part of keeping her able to cast her skills without fear of running out of either NRG or becoming CD-locked. For this build, this means that, pre-3-star, she can use [Portal] on turn 1, then use an [Instant Portal] on turn 2, followed by [Embrace of Shadows], then another [Portal] on turn 3. [Embrace of Shadows] on turn 4, then [Instant Portal], followed by [Portal] on turn 5 (assuming the bug mentioned above does not get fixed—we’ll see)! Post 3-star, she can actually double-[Portal] on turn 1, then use [Embrace of Shadows] on turn 2, and then double-[Portal] on turn 3 again, and can alternate like this endlessly (again, assuming the bug does not get patched, otherwise she will need to be 5-star to function in this way)! Equipment For a weapon, Yen’s best overall option for any build is (5-star, otherwise it is rather useless) Obsidian Star, her Astral Imprint; it can output similar damage to Diffusing Prism, with added utility in the form of allied debuff dispel and enemy buff dispel. If running her as a DPS, she can use Diffusing Prism, or if she is acting as a support, she can use a Focus Wand to extend the range of her [Portal] and [Charming Illusion] skills, or even a Reorganized Cube; any of the above can work for a mixed build. Otherwise, she can use a Starry Sky Heritage. For a trinket, if you are running her as a support or mixed build, I suggest Old Scrolls of Auguste, but Blood Luxite Amulet is a good choice for DPS builds (or even mixed builds). Shield of Justice can technically outdamage Blood Luxite Amulet by a slim margin, but is so highly contested as a trinket that I advise saving it for another character. Naturally, any trinket that boosts M.ATK is a great choice on her, as well. For a tarot, I suggest Magician, as it will allow her to do more damage and, even in her support build, she will appreciate the extra DMG it provides. If your weapon does not provide any kind of dispel, though, you might want to consider Empress. Otherwise, if you are desperate for more healing, you could consider High Priestess. For engravings, Sword + Wand is best, as Yen really does not benefit too much from Cup + Wand, even as a support, since she reduces her own CDs so well, and [Magic Surge] tends to waste its resonance effect anyway. When rolling stats, focus on M.ATK, with a secondary focus on HP. Additional Notes Below are the other skills that will be needed for Yen’s support and DPS builds. Charming Illusion Alt. Skill [Charming Illusion] allows Yen to create an immobile duplicate of herself on an empty tile within 4 tiles, which then inflicts [Bewitch] (which causes its targets to move uncontrollably toward the duplicate—this is another [Bound] effect) on all enemies within 2 tiles of the duplicate for 1 turn, and deals 80% DMG to them, and inflicts them [Vulnerable III] for 2 turns. When she has [Magic Boost], this skill becomes an [Instant] skill. The base version costs 2 NRG and has a CD of 3 turns. This is an excellent way to disrupt enemies and make them ripe for destruction! Elemental Control Alt. Skill [Elemental Control] is a 3-in-1 skill that gives Yen multiple offensive options for different situations. [Ball Lightning] is a single-target attack that does 170% DMG to an enemy. [Pyrokinesis] allows Yen to select a tile within 4 tiles in a cross-shape and deal 120% [Fire] AoE DMG to all targets within 5 horizontal tiles, and turns those tiles into [Burning] tiles for 2 turns. [Hail] deals 120% [Ice] AoE DMG to all targets within 2 tiles of the targeted enemy. When possessing [Magic Boost], this skill becomes a combination of all the elements, dealing 180% DMG to all enemies with 3 tiles of the target enemy, though the multiplier decreases by 15% per additional enemy hit, up to 45%. Before attacking, Yen’s ATK increases by 15% and her Crit increases by 50%, and she inflicts [Vulnerable III] on targets within 2 tiles of the central target for 2 turns. After she attacks, she then inflicts all targets with [Ignition] (“Debuff. Life Loss. At the end of the turn, loses HP equal to 25% of the sum of the caster’s P.ATK and M.ATK.”) and turns all affected tiles to [Frosty] tiles for 2 turns. All of the base skills cost 3 NRG and have no CD, so with proper NRG support, this skill can be spammed quite reliably. Yennefer's Mockery Alt. Skill [Yennefer’s Mockery] is a passive which simply increases Yen’s DMG dealt by 30% and increases it by another 30% against targets with any [Bound] effect. This is a great way to increase her damage output, especially with proper support! Yennefer’s support build is, ideally, [Portal], [Charming Illusion], and [Tissaia’s Teachings], and she will want to equip Obsidian Star or Focus Wand, Old Scrolls of Auguste, and Magician or Empress. The focus of this build is to spread as much [Vulnerable III] as possible, while also disrupting enemies with Yennefer’s illusion and teleporting her teammates around the battlefield. Her DPS build opts instead for [Embrace of Shadows], [Elemental Control], and (once she is 4-star) [Yennefer’s Mockery] ([Tissaia’s Teachings] otherwise), and she will want to equip Obsidian Star or Diffusing Prism, Blood Luxite Amulet (or any trinket that boosts M.ATK), and Magician. With this build, Yennefer will want to focus on causing as much destruction as possible. As an added bonus, she works well alongside Triss due to her ability to create both [Frosty] and [Burning] tiles with [Elemental Control]. Overall, Yennefer’s longevity will come from her mixed/support builds, more than anything else, due to her powerful buffs, debuffs, and unique utility effects, like what has happened with SP Inanna.
