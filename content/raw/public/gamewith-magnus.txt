Also, by activating his Skill [Cry of Victory], he can debuff enemies and buff allies, so you can gain an advantage in battle simply by getting close to enemies. Also has a Leader's Aura He has a Leader's Aura that greatly buffs characters of the Faction, allowing him to constantly buff his allies. The Fortitude Faction includes powerful characters like Gloria and Cocoa , so if you have them, try pairing them with him. Acts as Both Defender and Attacker As his P.DEF increases, so does his attack performance, allowing him to have both high durability and damage. His Role is Defender, but since he cannot directly protect allies, he performs more as an Attacker who deals high damage while inflicting debuffs. Poor Mobility Magnus is powerful as long as he can get close to enemies, but he has no movement skills and is vulnerable to height advantages, so support is essential. Make sure to secure a way for him to get close to enemies by using Tactical Skills, Gear, and other characters' Skills . Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Magnus Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ Magnus has a powerful and unique trait, making him a high priority for star level upgrades. At 3-stars, his attack and defense debuffs increase from -8% to -12%, boosting his support value. If you want to enhance him further, aim for 5-stars to extend the range of his debuffs, as well as increase their potency. Priority Skills Skill build Priority and Details Mountain Breaker ★★★★★ A single-target attack Skill that also triggers [Cry of Victory]. Since this Skill supports Magnus's strength, prioritize it for builds when utilizing [Cry of Victory]. Cry of Victory ★★★★★ A powerful conditional Skill that increases damage dealt by allies in a wide area and applies powerful debuffs that lower enemy attack all at once. Prioritize it for slots unless other allies can apply similar buffs/debuffs. Bloodthirsty Axe ★★★★★ A Reaction that allows Strike Back with Basic Attack. Therefore, with [Mighty Swing], it becomes an AoE attack, and with [Feverish Attack], Life Steal occurs. Since Magnus often positions himself in the front, use this Reaction in your build. Mighty Swing ★★★★★ A Basic Attack that can deal damage over a wide area. Slot when focusing on multi-target battles. Foreceful Cast ★★★★ A unique Skill that allows throwing enemies or some objects to distant places. It can neutralize troublesome enemies by throwing them off cliffs or into low ground, or deal massive AoE damage by throwing gunpowder barrels. Often useful, especially in multi-target battles. Blood & Steel ★★★★ A Leader's Aura that greatly strengthens allies. Since includes powerful characters like Cocoa and Gloria , include it if they are present in the party. Feverish Attack ★★★★ A Basic Attack with a Life Steal effect. Since it also enables Life Steal when Striking Back with [Bloodthirsty Axe], slot it when prioritizing durability. Secondary Priorities Skill build Priority and Details Hardening ★★★ If you want to reduce damage taken, use this Reaction in your build. Crushing Bash ★★★ An excellent Skill that inflicts a 20% increased damage taken debuff + deals 120% damage at no cost. Use when in single-target or boss battles when other characters cannot apply a similar debuff. Iron Chariot ★★ NRG consumption is high at 4, but it's a single-target attack Skill with very high damage dealt. Use in build when NRG Recovery is possible from other characters, or when lacking damage in single-target battles. Offensive Stance ★ Increases damage taken, but it's an Instant Skill that can add 40% of P.DEF to P.ATK. Use in build when you want to boost damage dealt. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Magnus Hollow Axe Feast Axe Undying Flames Since Magnus' Trait adds a portion of his P.DEF to his P.ATK when attacking, the [Hollow Axe] is an obvious choice for him. Otherwise, you could also use [Feast Axe] or [Brutal Axe] as both options work well with frontliners. Recommended Trinkets for Magnus Space-time Ring - - [Space-time Ring] simultaneously supplements Magnus' low movement and allows him to reliably protect allies. Alternatively, [Crystal Burst Armor] enhances Magnus' [Strike Back]s, and [Disaster Bottle] is a good choice for single-target battles. Recommended Tarots for Magnus Destruction of The Tower Temptation of The Devil Temptation of The Devil For [Strike Back] builds, [The Tower] synergizes well with Magnus against multiple enemies. [The Chariot] for enhanced Move and [The Devil] for offense/defense boosts are ideal. Recommended Engravings for Magnus Resonance Effect Sword Cup When actively attacking, increases the DMG by 5% and grants 15% of [Life Steal]. Magnus engravings can be minmaxed depending on how you intend to play him. If you want to lean towards offense, [Sword + Cup] is best for the [Life Steal]. If you're considering a [Strike Back] build, then [Wand + Pentacle] is recommended. If you just want Magnus to frontline, [Penatcle + Pentacle] is always a decent option. The fiercely loyal Marshal of the King's Army of Iria. Magnus is a veteran commander with a bold and strategic style of warframe and is said to embody the military soul of Iria. As the right hand of the king, he made great contributions during the War of Independence, and instructed the two princes in military strategy. Even after the king's retirement, he continued to defend the peace of Iria as a representative of the king's will and supporter of Prince Dantalion .
