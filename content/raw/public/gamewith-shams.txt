Note that he's not an easy character to use because his playstyle requires the management of buffs and unique mechanics, but if you can master him, he becomes an extremely powerful character . Viable Strike Back Character In addition to excelling as an attacker, Shams is also capable of using potent Strike Backs . It is also possible to be tanky while dealing significant damage, so you can expect Shams to shine as an active and reliable front-liner. Powerful Single-Target Strike Backs [Gladiator's Strike] is the core of the Strike Back build since it applies [The Challenger] debuff to the enemy it's used on. This causes all Strike Backs to target that enemy. Instead of striking back at each enemy who attacks Shams, use this to focus your attention and continuously Strike Back a troublesome enemy . Requires Managing Multiple Buffs Skills that grant [Liberation] Trait Skill: Sigil - Full Liberation Gathered Radiance Skills that grant [Poised and Ready] Dawn's Resurgence Gathered Radiance Solar Piercing Skills that Apply [The Challenger] Everburning Sun Gladiator's Strike Shams can enhance the performance of his main skills with [Liberation] stacks and the [Poised and Ready] buff. Whenever an enemy is afflicted with [The Challenger], Shams gains additional effects, increased damage, and reduced damage taken. It's essential to plan ahead with Skill usage and their effects while considering your party composition and the stage. Unique Survivability Mechanic [Sigil - Full Liberation] is automatically used to grant Shams [Liberation] stacks and a Shield, but at the same time, the [Sigil Overdraw] debuff sets his HP to 30%, and he can no longer recover above that. While this effect is active, be careful as he becomes vulnerable to Piercing DMG and DoT effects that ignore Shields. Stars/Passives Can Reduce Drawbacks The limited HP effect can be removed by reaching 4★ or by equipping [Imperial Blood Rite]. Once the recovery limit is removed, Shams survivability is substantially increased thanks to the Shield's effect. Adjust Build According to Supports Shams reaches his true potential under certain conditions, so it's important to set the stage for Shams to shine. Make it easier to access bonus effects by deploying characters like Lukamar who can grant Liberation, or by increasing Shams' ★. Depending on the availability of support and his star level, it's also important to decide which Passive Skills to equip. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Shams Aim to Reach 2★ → 4★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 2★ → 4★ → 5★ As early as 2★, Shams gains a power boost since the duration of Sigil Overdraw is reduced by 1 turn. His next spike starts at 4★, which removes the HP restriction from Sigil Overdraw. Finally, at 5★, Sigil Overdraw's duration is further reduced and also increases its benefits. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Everburning Sun ★★★★★ A Skill that deals overwhelming damage under the right conditions. Be mindful of removing the [Prepared] status with the [Poised and Ready] buff, and increasing damage with 10 stacks of [Liberation]. Sigil's Protection ★★★★★ A Reaction that can significantly reduce DMG taken. Despite having conditions such as having a Shield and when actively attacked, Shams can easily utilize this ability. Solar Piercing ★★★★★ If you can reduce the turns of [Sigil Overdraw] via Star Levels or have other characters grant [Liberation], this Passive greatly increases the opportunities for [Poised and Ready]. However, if your Star Levels are low or you can't apply [Liberation] with other characters, we recommend equipping other Passives. Sandfall Breach ★★★★★ A Basic Attack with conditional bonus effects. These conditions are generally easy to meet with Elaman units, so you should equip this over the other attack for more damage. Earthshatter Blaze ★★★★ A Skill that can apply a -60% DEF debuff while moving. Be careful as the attack range is a 3x2 area in front of the landing spot, so it can be hard to properly land the attack in some situations. It becomes [Instant ]with 10 stacks of [Liberation], so it's very powerful if you can maintain 10 stacks. Gladiator's Strike ★★★★ The core Skill for a [Strike Back] build. The more [Gladiator Supreme] buffs you have, the better the performance of [Strike Back], so it's essential to be mindful about obtaining [Poised and Ready], as well as 10 stacks of [Liberation]. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Imperial Blood Rite ★★★ An excellent Passive that enhances a Reaction, improves [Sigil Overdraw] and increases survivability. This skill makes Shams significantly more durable, so prioritize equipping it with a [Strike Back] build. Dawn's Resurgence ★★ A Skill that can remove the [Prepared] status from allies and self. When applied to Shams, it enables a [Re-attack], but its CD is long. If you're only thinking of strengthening Shams, you'll prefer other excellent Passives. So only equip this when it's useful to remove the [Prepared] status from other characters. Gathered Radiance ★★ A Passive that grants [Poised and Ready] and 5 stacks of [Liberation]. If your Star Levels are low and you can't use [Sigil - Full Liberation] often, or when you can't apply [Liberation] with other characters, equipping this Skill makes using Shams more comfortable. Liberation - Toughness ★ You're prone to becoming [Injured] due to [Sigil - Full Liberation], but the other Reaction is more effective, so use the other one in most cases. Feverish Attack ★ You don't have many opportunities to use a Basic Attack, and the damage dealt tends to be higher with the other Basic Attack, so this has a low priority. Desert Warrior ★ There are numerous other Passives with better effects, so this has a low build priority. Insight ★ There are numerous other Passives with better effects, so this has a low build priority. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Shams Undying Flames Brutal Axe Tuning Hammer Equip Shams with Weapons that increase his offensive stats, such as Undying Flames. Due to [Sigil - Full Liberation], it's easy to maintain low HP, which synergizes well with Brutal Axe. However, be aware that at 4★ or higher, or when using [Imperial Blood Rite], the healing restriction from [Sigil Overdraw] is removed. Recommended Trinkets for Shams Memory of Teeth Fafnir the Scarlet Armor Crystal Burst Armor We recommend Gear that increase stats, such as Memory of Teeth, Fafnir, and Maverick's Cloak. For Strike Back builds, Crystal Burst Armor is also an excellent choice. Recommended Tarots for Shams Verdict of Justice Hierophant's Laws Temptation of The Devil If equipped with Undying Flames, Justice is the most recommended. If you don't have it, Gear a Tarot such as Hierophant or Devil . Also, for a Strike Back build, Tower is also an option. Recommended Engravings for Shams Resonance Effect Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Sword Cup When actively attacking, increases the DMG by 5% and grants 15% of [Life Steal]. Wand Wand After using a [basic attack], [skill], [strike back], or [assisting attack], gains 1 random [Level 2 Attribute Buff]. Combinations like Sword+Wand to increase stats, Wand+Wand for buffs, and Sword+Cup for Life Steal + DMG are most recommended. Shams's Skin Initial Ascended ▲ Unlock Ascended Skin when character reaches Star Level 5. Shams is a prince of Elaman and Lukamar's younger brother. His presence on the battlefield strikes like thunder, earning him recognition as Elaman's greatest warrior. As a young man, Shams knelt before the Elaman imperial mausoleum and swore to give everything for his homeland's restoration. From that day forward, he fought countless campaigns for Elaman and became the nation's undisputed hero. After the Second Radiant War, the Grand Vizier chose to cooperate w ith the Papal States and keep a low profile. The fiery young Shams couldn't stomach this, gathering passionate nationalist youths to form the Sunfure Corps. When border conflicts erupted between Elaman and the Papal States, the Sunfire Corps steamrolled through enemy territory, winning battle after battle—but their victories pushed both nations toward all-out war. Eventually, the Grand Vizier conspired with the Papal States to wipe out the Sunfire Corps entirely, leaving countless soldiers buried in desert graves. This devastating defeat stripped Shams of his political power. Though he could no longer shape policy, his dream of restoring Elaman burned as bright as ever, and his hatred for the Papal States had been seared into his very soul. He chose to wait in the shadows for the moment when the sun would truly rise again...
