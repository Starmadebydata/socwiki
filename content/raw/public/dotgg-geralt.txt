Never trust those things. But now that he’s here, he’s ready to lay waste to our enemies with a kit as versatile as they come. With tools from AoE and single-target physical attacks, to shielding, knockback, and debuffing in his kit, as well as a powerful leader aura, Geralt has all the tools and more that justify his place on just about any team! He has a few different builds; I’ll focus primarily on the one you will want to bring for most content (his physical AoE build), but also briefly touch on other options in the Additional Notes section below. Geralt of Rivia Trait Geralt’s trait, [Geralt of Rivia], firstly sets his M.ATK to always be qual to his P.ATK. No buffing, debuffing, etc. will have any effect on his M.ATK; it will always be equal to whatever his current P.ATK stat is in battle. He also gains a skill in battle called [Witcher Potions], which allows him to use your choice from four different potions, each of which is an [Instant] skill. The first potion is [Swallow], which restores 35% of Geralt’s HP, and gives him the [Swallow] buff (which heals him for 35% of his HP at the end of his turn) for 3 turns. The second potion is [Thunderbolt], which gives him the [Thunderbolt] buff for 3 turns (which increases his P.ATK by 20%, which stacks with traditional ATK buffs). The third potion is [Tawny Owl], which restores 1 NRG and gives him the [Tawny Owl] buff (which gives him 1 NRG whenever he uses a [Sign Skill] or ends his turn) for 3 turns. The fourth and final potion is [Maribor Forest], which gives him the [Maribor Forest] buff, which increases his Crit by 30% (which stacks with other Crit buffs) for 3 turns. Only 2 of these skills can be active at any given moment, and, in most cases, you will want [Thunderbolt] and [Maribor Forest] active. The good news is Geralt gets 100% uptime on these buffs! As for the rest of his trait, at 1-star, it merely affords him an additional 10% Crit. By the time he reaches 5-star, that number goes up to 30%, and he also gains an additional movement (at 3-star), and even reduces all of his NRG costs by 1. He also gains [Regeneration I-II] (for end of turn passive heals) and [DMG Reduction I-II] whenever he drinks any potion, and, whenever he hits a target with either a physical or magical attack, he then inflicts the corresponding defensive debuff on any enemies hit (either [▼ P.DEF I-II] or [▼ M.DEF I-II]) for 3 turns! Lastly, he is also given 1-2 stacks of [Quen Shield] (“Gains Physical Shield equal to 15% of HP, up to 4 stacks. When broken, deals 35% magical AoE DMG to all enemies within 3 tiles and knocks the adjacent targets back by 1 tile. When multiple stacks are broken at once, effects stack.”) at the start of the battle. Skill Recommendations Powerful Attack Basic Attack Geralt’s preferred basic attack is [Powerful Attack]. Realistically, you’ll probably never be using it, but the extra damage is better than a situational debuff that most difficult enemies are immune to anyway. Fleet Footed Reaction Skill Geralt’s unique reaction skill, [Fleet Footed], gives him 20% DMG reduction, plus an additional 20% DMG reduction against ranged attacks. Once per turn, when targeted by a single-target attack, he gains one stack of [Dodge], and dodges the attack. This skill also gives him the ability to perform [Assisting Cover] (swapping places right before the attack hits) for other Dimensional Traveler allies within 3 tiles of himself, which works nicely with his dodge, and his exploding Quen shield(s). Finisher: Whirl Skill 1 [Finisher: Whirl] is simple, but deadly; it is a 3-hit, 50% DMG per hit, attack that hits all enemies within 3 tiles of Geralt. Effectively, this means 150% DMG, which is significant, but it is made to shine by its large area of effect and the fact that it only costs 3 NRG, which becomes only 2 NRG at 5-star, with absolutely no CD. That means that any time he gets any kind of bonus action, he can reuse this skill as much as he wants, as long as he has enough NRG, which is easily possible through various supports, Urgent Order, or, even in the worst case scenario, [Tawny Owl] (though I am not a fan of missing out on either 20% ATK or 30% Crit for this, so I suggest other supports). Yrden: Spin Attack Skill 2 While Geralt has access to 5 different [Sign Skills], the one that best synergizes with [Finisher: Whirl] is most often going to be [Yrden: Spin Attack]. This skill is an [Instant] skill which inflicts all targets within 3 tiles of Geralt with [▼Move II], [▼SPD II], and [Vulnerable II] for 2 turns, before dealing 2 hits of 30% physical AoE DMG to those targets. This skill is the perfect setup for [Finisher: Whirl], given the Vulnerable debuff and the physical defense debuff it inflicts (from his trait at 4-star+) to the targets, softening them up massively for the follow-up blow. Just a caveat with [Sign Skills]—if you use one, all [Sign Skills] are forced into a 1 turn CD that you cannot reduce, so Geralt can only use one sign per turn, no matter what, though they never cost any NRG. The other signs will be discussed in the Additional Notes section below. Bonds of Kaer Morhen Skill 3 For his final skill, [Bonds of Kaer Morhen], his leader aura, is the best option. It provides the usual ATK and DEF buffs to all Dimensional Traveler allies, as well as increases their DMG and piercing DMG by 10%, and the damage they deal to any enemies inflicted with any [Bound] status by 20%. Given how the Witcher collab team centers around the [Stagger] status, this aura will serve them very well for boosting damage output significantly. Equipment For a weapon, unsurprisingly, the best option is Geralt’s astral gear, Witcher’s Swords, because it simply provides a huge ATK boost, but Void Stab is pretty much equally as good, though it has a slower startup, and Blade of Stratagem can actually outdamage either of them, though has less synergy with Act Again, since he will not be able to implant debuffs via Yrden on additional targets. For a trinket, as usual, you will want the best P.ATK-boosting one you can give him. Shield of Justice does have great synergy with his [Quen Shield], as long as your goal is not to get it popped. Blood Luxite Amulet is great for either a Quen build or an Igni-centered build. For a tarot, Magician is the best option for his AoE build, and works well even for the single-target build (since it’s not truly single-target), but Justice also works well on him, and is a strong second, and Devil is too, as long as you can reliably build stacks on him early. For engravings, definitely go with Sword + Wand, and when rolling stats, focus on P.ATK, with HP as the preferred secondary stat. Additional Notes You can also opt for a more single-target build on Geralt, which only changes Skill 1 and Skill 2 to [Finisher: Rend] and either [Axii: Execution] or [Aard Sign], respectively. Finisher: Rend Alt. Skill [Finisher: Rend] is another simple skill which deals 180% physical AoE DMG to a 1x4 area in a straight line and, before attacking, increases Geralt’s ATK by a massive 40%! The area of effect for this skill is less-than-ideal, but whatever it does hit is liable to melt, especially in light of some of the other [Sign Skills] he can cast before using this. To make this great skill even better, just like [Finisher: Whirl], it costs only 3 NRG, which is reduced to 2 NRG at 5-star, meaning it’s perfectly spammable. Aard Sign Alt. Skill [Aard Sign] is my main suggestion for the [Sign Skill] on the single-target build. It has two different forms: [Aard: Blast] and [Aard: Storm]. [Aard: Blast] is an [Instant] skill which fires a blast of force in a 1x3 area in the selected direction, and knocks back the first target by 2 tiles (if on the last tile, they only get knocked back by 1 tile, which leaves them in range of [Finisher: Rend]!). If the target is an item, he deals 100% DMG to it. All targets in the area then take 50% increased DMG from the next ATK with which Geralt hits them within the current turn, which will almost certainly be [Finisher: Rend]! Alternatively, you can use [Aard: Storm], which knocks back the first enemy in each column within a 3x3 area by 4(!) tiles, and damages any affected items for 300% DMG. Realistically it’s the [Instant] skill you mainly want, but there’s no denying the potential usefulness of [Aard: Storm] in all kinds of situations where knockback is king. Axii: Execution Alt. Skill Alternatively, you can bring [Axii: Execution], which is an [Instant] skill that targets an adjacent foe, inflicting them with [Stagger] (“Debuff. Becomes [Stunned] for 1 turn at the start of the turn.” Even if the target is immune to [Bound] effects, they will still be staggered and be counted as [Bound] for the sake of boosted damage from the leader aura.), [⦸ Passive Skills], and [⦸ Reaction Skills] for 2 turns, and then deals 70% physical DMG. While the synergy with his leader aura is there, it’s honestly better to run Aard on him, and allow Ciri to be the one to implant [Stagger], as the boost from Aard is simply much more significant, and has no other source. This can, however, be great for shutting down problematic passives and/or reactions, so keep this in your back pocket for frustrating, gimmicky enemies (like Brutebears and their healing transformations!). The last two [Sign Skills] are worth mentioning, though their uses are more niche. Igni Sign Alt. Skill [Igni Sign], like [Aard Sign], is two skills in one: [Igni: Rotation] and [Igni: Firestream]. [Igni: Rotation] is an [Instant] skill that deals 20% Fire AoE DMG to all enemies within 3 tiles of Geralt, and also causes his attacks that turn to do an additional 20% magical AoE DMG. It also gives him [Armor Piercing], which allows him to ignore 40% of the target’s DEF (this becomes less useful when debuffing DEF), for the rest of the turn. The biggest issue with this skill (as opposed to Yrden) for the AoE build is that it inflicts an M.DEF debuff, rather than a P.DEF debuff, since the initial hit is magical, meaning it does not work as well to set up [Finisher: Whirl] as Yrden does, though it could be used to set up a mage team, perhaps? [Igni: Firestream] is a normal skill which deals 4 hits of 40% Fire AoE DMG to all enemies within a 3x3 area in front of him and, before attacking, his ATK increases by 40%, making this his best magical DPS option, if you are so inclined. Quen Sign Alt. Skill Last but not least, [Quen Sign] is yet another two-in-one skill. The first skill is [Quen: Swiftguard], which is an [Instant] skill that gives Geralt 1 stack of [Quen Shield]. The other option is [Quen: Fortified], which gives him 4 stacks of [Quen Shield], but is a normal skill. Very simple, but effective if you’re trying to build your Geralt to be tanky and/or centered around countering, though I do not feel this is the best use of Geralt. Do note that if you go this route, Shield of Justice becomes a less viable option, since you actually want his shields to pop.
