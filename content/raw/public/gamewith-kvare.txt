Because these damage types affect the target's HP directly, Kvare is very effective against units with high defences and/or shields. Unique Roster Debuff How to Apply Roster ・Use the [Roster] Additional Skill ・Passively applied to the nearest enemy within 5 spaces at the end of each turn. Details ・Lasts for 3 turns. ・At the start of the turn, inflicts two random [Level 2 Debuffs] on the target for 2 turns. ・Additional effect when [Roster] expires or when the target enters the [Dying] state. ∟ Receive 100% of Kvare's Magic Attack as Piercing DMG. ∟[Roster] is dispelled once additional effect triggers. ・Removed whenever [This Is the Moment for Rain] is used. ・Whenever [Roster] is removed, Kvare's ATK and DEF go up by 5%. (Max 20%) For Kvare, playing around his unique [Roster] debuff is pivotal to playing this character as effectively as possible. By removing and re-applying [Roster], Kvare can deal additional damage as well as self-strengthen . Hits Multiple Targets When [This Is the Moment for Rain] is enhanced with the [Wither] passive skill, Kvare can deal Piercing DMG and apply Life Loss over a wide area . Additionally, enemies already affected by [Roster] will receive various debuffs and additional damage. Cover Kvare with Allies When aiming to hit as many enemies as possible with Kvare's skills, he will often position himself within the range of multiple enemies. Defend Kvare with skills like Cocoa and Maitha's [Assisting Cover] , or mitigate some damage by using [Kvare's Gift]. Effective Even Against Bosses Kvare alone can apply a large number of numerous debuffs on a single target thanks to [Roster] and [Unforgettable Erosion] . What's more, there are also builds that allow the stacking of [Life Loss], causing large amounts of damage every turn. Extremely High Damage Potential By heavily investing into improving Kvare's M.ATK, depending on the build, it is possible to deal tens of thousands of damage by stacking [Life Loss] . Be Wary of Immunities Kvare's strength lies in his ability to apply a large number of different debuffs, therefore, enemes who that have immunities or dispelling capabilities will make him less effective . While there are only a few examples of enemies that can do this, make sure to pay attention to things like stage mutations and character abilities. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Kvare Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ At 3-stars, Kvare’s skill [Roster] refunds 1 EN, and at 5-stars, it refunds all EN and gains additional uses. These upgrades greatly increase Kvare's action economy in multi-enemy battles, so if you're planning to run Kvare, prioritizing his star upgrades is highly recommended. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Conviction ★★★★★ With 0 CD, this Skill can be used every turn to inflict powerful debuffs and [Life Loss]. For enemies possessing the [Roster] debuff, some of its effects change, and it will deal 100% magic damage. Very powerful against single targets. Infectious Attack ★★★★★ Although Basic Attacks are rarely used, this Basic Attack, which can deal continuous damage through [Infection], is recommended. This Is the Moment for Rain ★★★★★ Deals Piercing DMG to a wide area, and if [Roster] is applied, it removes it to inflict additional damage and debuffs like Piercing + Vulnerable. When paired with [Wither], the effect changes and the damage increases significantly—so it’s generally best to equip them together. Wither ★★★★★ A powerful Skill that enhances [This Is the Moment for Rain], as well as allows you to inflict [Life Loss] when using an active Skill. It's powerful even iwithout [This Is the Moment for Rain], so prioritize equipping it. Unforgettable Erosion ★★★★ A passive that inflicts Attribute Debuffs and [Life Loss] to surrounding enemies every turn. Combined with the Trait [Roster], Kvare can significantly weaken enemies alone while also dealing continuous damage, making it very powerful. Entrapping ★★★★ A Reaction that reduces damage and steals Attribute Buffs. On stages without AoE attacks, slot this. Sanctuary Emblem ★★★★ A Reaction that, after taking AoE damage, grants a Shield of the same damage type to all surrounding allies. The effect is very powerful, but it's useless in stages without AoE attacks, so be careful. Also, the effect can only trigger once for physical and once for magical damage respectively. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Kvare's Gift ★★★ Can only be used once, but it's an excellent Skill with a damage sharing effect. Can also be used on enemies to inflict [Stun]. Since Kvare tends to fight on the front lines, you'll want to utilize this Skill and [Assisting Cover] to fight. Will of Victory ★★ A special Skill that changes each time it's used. It has many useful effects like full HP recovery, full NRG recovery, and large single-target damage. Note that each skill can only be used once. Therefore, you should only equip it when you understand the enemy's action patterns and have a clear purpose for each of its effects. Victory Before Battle ★★ A passive that, when [Roster] is removed, recovers HP & NRG and grants a Lv2 buff. If your Star Level is low, equip this when you can't upkeep NRG. Silencing Attack ★ Difficult to trigger, and few situations where it's needed. However, the effect itself is powerful, so it can be viable in some niche stages. The [Diffusing Prism] works especially well in fights against multiple enemies, while the [Nightmare Puppet] is a great pick for single-target situations. Recommended Trinkets for Kvare Shield of Justice Origin Hourglass Wild Spice Vial For Kvare, the [Shield of Justice] and [Origin Hourglass] are especially recommended, as they significantly boost magic attack and enhance the effectiveness of his [Life Loss] skill. Kvare's Skin Initial Ascended ▲ Unlock Ascended Skin when character reaches Star Level 5. Calm and wise, Kvare, always in control, is the wealthiest and most influential noble in Iria today. During the War of Independence, Kvare was one of King Faris's most important comrades-in-arms. As a representative of the Old Nobles, he influenced many nobles to side with Faris, adding a heavyweight to the victory of the War of Independence. Now, as the Minister of Finance of the kingdom, no matter how the situation changes, the far-sighted Kvare can always find the most advantageous response strategy for Iria, and for himself. Just like the sudden changes in Waverun City in 992; it may not be beyond Kvare's expectations... When he was young, Kvare also had a tender love affair and had an illegitimate daughter named Safiyyah .
