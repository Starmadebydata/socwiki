In tile tactics games, having high movement is a universally useful tool that allows you to chase fleeing targets, reach checkpoints in time, or even position your characters for better attacking angles. Note! As an additional passive effect, Flag Waving increases the attack and defense of nearby allies, making Gloria's support performance extremely high right from the start of the battle. Powerful All-Around Unit Auras, Buffs, and Utilities In addition to Gloria's Trait, she also has many powerful support effects such as her Union faction Aura, and her [Lance of Longinus] ability. This ability allows Gloria to plant her Lance in an area to deal AoE damage, and grant nearby allies increased NRG regen. Pro Tip! The summoned spear becomes an object that can be attacked by the enemy. Use it strategically, such as positioning it to block allies or employing it as a decoy. Changes to Attacker With Potential Burst By using the RK 3 skill [Lance of Longinus], Gloria will gain access to a different set of skills that are dependent on the skills you originally had equipped. Gloria will also gain the Potential Burst buff, greatly increasing her attack and defense. Potential Burst coupled with the newly acquired offensive skills makes Gloria capable of dealing significant amounts of burst damage to enemies. Recommended Skill Rotation Support Allies using [Longinus - Brilliance] (Spear Mode) Enter Potential Burst (Sword Mode) by using [Lance of Longinus] Engage as an attacker and deal damage (Sword Mode) Support again by using [Longinus - Flag Waving] (Spear Mode) The most basic skill rotation is detailed above. Using both spear and sword skills takes some getting used to, but with practice, you can adapt to different roles, from support to frontline attacker. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Gloria Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ After switching to Sword Mode, Gloria gains +2 movement for 2 turns at 3-stars, and at 5-star, her attacks ignore 50% of the target’s defence. Both upgrades are extremely powerful, so she’s a high priority for Memory Shard farming and investment. Priority Skills Skill Build Priorityand Details Longinus - Brilliance ★★★★★ In "Spear Mode", it supports allies; in "Sword Mode", it transforms into a powerful AoE Skill. Prioritize slotting it since it excels in both offense and defense. Lance of Longinus ★★★★★ Summons a Spear that buffs all allies nearby allies. In "Spear Mode", it's an essential Skill that increases the ATK/DEF and Move of nearby allies. Since you cannot switch to Sword Mode without this Skill, it is considered essential . Front Defense ★★★★★ In high-difficulty stages, taking a single hit can be fatal, so this Reaction, which increases survivability, is highly recommended. Lance of Justice ★★★★★ In Spear Mode , it's an AoE attack; in Sword Mode , it's a Basic Attack whose power increases when attacking from the front. Generally, this Basic Attack is recommended, but its only drawback is the low damage in Spear Mode . Longinus - Thrust ★★★★ In Spear Mode , it's an easy-to-use knockback Skill; in Sword Mode , it transforms into a powerful single-target attack Skill that requires no cost, with damage increases based on the distance moved. You'll want to equip this if you don't have many [Union] characters in your party. Vow of Justice ★★★★ A Leader's Aura that greatly buffs [Union] characters. If you don't have [Union] characters other than Gloria in your lineup, you could swap it with [Longinus - Thrust]. Secondary Priorities Skill Build Priority and Details Counterattack ★★★ A Reaction that allows you to Strike Back when taking damage. In the early game or when farming low-difficulty stages, it's more recommended than [Front Defense]. Silencing Attack ★★★ Compared to [Lance of Justice], it deals higher damage even in Spear Mode , and its ability to apply debuffs to [Injured] enemies is pretty good. Knightly Spirit ★★ Since you gain increased damage dealt/decreased damage taken from the front, it's viable to slot it against bosses. Counterattack Mode ★ It's powerful when combined with [Counterattack], but since it prevents you from equipping other essential Skills, its priority is low. Fair Duel ★ It has the powerful effect of dispelling debuffs & enemy buffs, but since this role is generally better left to other characters, its equip priority is low. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Gloria Double-Sided Halberd Nameless Staff The Silent Guard The [Double-Sided Halberd], which greatly increases area damage works well with Gloria's various AoE skills. Other alternatives like the [Nameless Staff], which boosts movement and makes positioning easier, and the [Silent Guard], which enhances durability are also viable options. Recommended Trinkets for Gloria Old Scrolls of Auguste Disaster Bottle Flying Blade Armguard [Old Scrolls of Auguste] is recommended since it can manipulate the turn order while granting buffs by taking advantage of Gloria's relatively high SPD. Since there is no single best-in-slot Trinket, you should equip an easy-to-use Trinket with an [Instant] Skill, a Trinket that increases durability, or similar items. Recommended Tarots for Gloria Dream of The Magician Rays of the Sun Temptation of The Devil Outside of high-difficulty stages where reducing damage taken is crucial, Tarots that boost damage dealt—starting with [The Magician]—are recommended. Otherwise, [Judgement] is also a viable alternative for utility. Recommended Engravings for Gloria Resonance Effect Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Sword Cup When actively attacking, increases the DMG by 5% and grants 15% of [Life Steal]. Wand Cup At the end of the turn, there is a 50% chance to gain [Engraving Resonance]. Effect: When using an [active skill] next time, refunds the NRG consumed and resets the CD. The skill effect has a CD of 4 turns. (Does not trigger in real-time combat. The effect changes to increasing HP, ATK and DEF by 5%) Despite having access to buff skills, Gloria is still considered to be a powerful attacker, making engravings such as [Sword + Wand] and [Sword + Cup] her best options. Wielder of the Lance of Longinus and one of the youngest knights to hold the title of Paladin. Gloria possesses a noble character, befitting the scion of a family of knights, a heart filled with courage, and a dedication to justice. These qualities have made her a Union banner-bearer and the epitome of knighthood that young knights aspire to. After the tragedy in Waverun City, she was sent by the Council to Iria. She has sworn to fulfil her mission and eradicate evil so that justice may prevail.
