By combining the [Heart of Alert] skill you get at RK 11 and the appropriate gear, you can inflict significant out-of-turn damage during the enemy's turn with Alert skills. Use Appropriate Alert Skills The RK 3 Alert Skill [Arrow of Guardian Convallaria] has a wider range, deals damage only once, and interupts enemy actions. On the other hand, the RK 7 [Alert Short] does not interrupt actions but can attack up to two times. Use them according to the situation. Excellent Debuffs His Trait can apply debuffs every time you deal damage, so he can contribute in battle against bosses even without using Alert skills. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Faycal Aim to Reach 3★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 3★ → 5★ At 3-stars, Faycal's debuffs become guaranteed, and at 5-stars, his crit rate is boosted significantly. Since Faycal has a strong trait, you should aim for 3-star first. If you don’t have a higher-priority character to invest in, going for 5-stars is also worth considering. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Faycal Irian Combat Bow Cornucopia Longbow Melee Mega Crossbow Equip the [Irian Combat Bow] when using mainly [Alert] Skills or the [Cornucopia Longbow] against single-target bosses. The [Melee Mega Crossbow] is viable for [Strike Back] builds, but Faycal is not typically a character that should take hits. Recommended Trinkets for Faycal Hunter's Intuition True Lens Crystal Burst Armor When using [Alert] Skills, equip [Hunter's Intuition]. For single-target bosses or a balanced approach, use [True Lens]. If focusing on [Strike Back], equip [Crystal Burst Armor]. Recommended Tarots for Faycal Destruction of The Tower Rays of the Sun Temptation of The Devil If you're using [Alert] Skills or [Strike Back], [The Tower] is recommended. Otherwise, equip Tarots that increase your damage output. A young man with a troubled past and one of the initial members of the Sword of Convallaria. As a child, Faycal witnessed the destruction of his home. Despite his supportive relationship with Maitha and Rawiyah , he could not completely eliminate the darkness in his heart. For this reason, Faycal cherishes everything he has now while also being wary of outsiders. He vows to grow up and protect his family, even if it means sacrificing everything.
