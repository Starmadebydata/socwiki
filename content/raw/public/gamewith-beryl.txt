Numerous Damaging Skills Beryl is a powerful magic DPS with an initial skill that has an Armor Piercing effect. Additionally, Beryl has access to plenty of wide range skills, making her excellent at dealing damage from a distance. She is useful in any situation from minor skirmishes to boss battles. Consistently Casts Skills With the RK 3 passive skill 'NRG Restoration' and her Trait, Beryl restores enough NRG per turn to spam her abilties. This allows Beryl to use many high-damage skills, which is her primary strength. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Beryl Aim to Reach 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 5★ Increasing Beryl to 5-stars guarantees a buff and additional NRG recovery each turn. At 4-stars or less, this effect is only a probability instead of a guarantee, so if you are interested in using Beryl as a standalone DPS, make sure to get her to 5-stars. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Chuu Chuu Chuu! ★★★★★ A very powerful single-target Skill that ignores 40% of the target's defense. Prioritize equipping it as it's a main Skill that can be used every turn due to [NRG Restoration]. NRG Restoration ★★★★★ Prioritize equipping since when combined with Beryl's Trait, it allows for the recovery of a lot of NRG. Cutie ★★★★★ The effects is very potent as it suppresses ranged damage from magic or arrows, which is a common weakness of mages. Also excellent for reducing AoE damage from Bosses. Scorching Attack ★★★★★ A Basic Attack that can inflict Scorch for damage over time along with an attack. Better than the alternative basic attacks in most cases. BOOOOOM! ★★★★★ A powerful Skill that deals the highest single-target/AoE damage due to multi-hit damage. The drawback is how often you can use this Skill, so support Beryl with NRG Recovery Skills. It can also be used after moving by using an [Instant] skill such as those granted by Trinkets. (I.e, Flying Blade Armguard) Flaming Meteor ★★★★ Powerful when combined with instant activation through [Act Again] or Stunned immunity through Bound Immunity. Equip when seeking attack range. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Wall of Flame ★★★ Creates Burning tiles that deal damage over time. Can also be used for disruption against enemies, so you'll want to equip it in stages with narrow passages or where DoTs are effective. Try This! ★★ Equipping it is an option whenever multiple allies need NRG. However, since Beryl herself is a powerful attacker, it's better to act as an attacker rather than a supporter. Bang! ★★ Has long range but cannot attack adjacent enemies. Deals physical damage, so it's effective against enemies with high M.DEF and unsuitable for enemies with high P.DEF. Equipping it is an option in stages with many enemies with high P.DEF. Firewalking ★ Priority is low because of its limited use cases and its effects are negligible. First Aid ★ Only equip if your account is lacking a dedicated healer. Otherwise, bring another character who is more proficient in healing, such as Inanna , Samantha , or Angel . Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Beryl Diffusing Prism Focus Wand Notes of A Genius [Diffusing Prism] and [Starry Sky Heritage], which enhance both single-target and AoE attacks, are especially recommended for maximizing overall combat efficiency in Sword of Convallaria. However, if you have neither one of them, alternatives like [Focus Wand] or [Resentment Broom] are both viable, particularly for builds that lean towards either single-target of AoE. Recommended Trinkets for Beryl Disaster Bottle Spring Pill Blood Luxite Amulet The powerful Skill [BOOOOOM!] hits hard against both single and multiple targets, but it usually can't be used after moving. To get around this, activate an [Instant] Skill from a Trinket after repositioning — that’ll let you unleash it right away, making it best to equip a Trinket with a strong [Instant] Skill to maximize Beryl's damage potential. Recommended Tarots for Beryl Dream of The Magician Hierophant's Laws Temptation of The Devil For all but the most challenging stages where you want to minimize incoming damage, Tarots that boost your damage output, such as [The Magician], are recommended. Recommended Engravings for Beryl Resonance Effect Sword Pentacle When [unharmed], increases ATK and DEF by 12% . Sword Cup When actively attacking, increases the DMG by 5% and grants 15% of [Life Steal]. Wand Cup At the end of the turn, there is a 50% chance to gain [Engraving Resonance]. Effect: When using an [active skill] next time, refunds the NRG consumed and resets the CD. The skill effect has a CD of 4 turns. (Does not trigger in real-time combat. The effect changes to increasing HP, ATK and DEF by 5%) When using Beryl as a DPS, we recommend engraving [Sword + Cup] or [Sword + Pentacle] to increase the overall damage she can deal. [Sword + Pentacle] is easier to maintain when Beryl is equipped with the [Focus Wand] weapon, since both have similar triggering conditions. However, note that losing one bonus will result in losing the other, making Beryl weaker if no one can heal her. A genius little Sorceress. Beryl is as talkative and qurious as she is quirky, and is very passionate about Luxite research. From an early age, Beryl underwent human Luxite experiments, which stunted her physical and mental growth. This is why she appears much younger than her actual age. After an accident occurred where she was able to escape, she became a wandering Sorceress — sought after by the Papal States for her strong affinity for Luxite research. She decided to settle in to the Town of Convallaria, of which she is very fond. She opened a Luxite Workshop to continue her research, and is constantly pursuing her desire to solve the mysteries of the world.
