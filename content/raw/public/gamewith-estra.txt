These conditions can all stack with one another, granting Estra a significant amount damage. Under the right conditions, Estra becomes the character with the highest, and most consistent single-target damage ceiling in the game . High Maneuverability and Re-Attacks Estra's movement potential is innately high due to her impressive Move and Jump stats. Estra's excellent maneuverability is further compounded with skills like [Shadow Blitz] and [No Restraints] , which both have a respectable range of up to 3 tiles away. Furthermore, defeating an enemy with [Shadow Blitz] grants Estra a [Re-attack], allowing her to use multiple actions in a single turn, and be active in all directions of the map . Attack Increases with Stats Stat. Increase Effects HP The maximum value of Magical Shield scales with Estra's max HP , which subsequently increases Estra's P.ATK due to her Trait effects. P.ATK Not only increases Estra's damage, but also increases the damage boost received from [Water of Exorcism] and [Stance of Exorcism] . M.DEF Increases the amount of Magical Shield generated every turn from Estra's Trait. Estra can increase the potency of her Trait and Skills by increasing her HP, P.ATK, and M.DEF, so aim for increasing these stats with Gear and Engravings . Specifically, pay attention to Estra's HP stat, which increases her Magic Shield, therefore making it possible to balance both offense and defense . Self Buffs with [Potion] Decision Skill Effect Blood of Shadows Usable once, grants [Dark Devour] Increases Crit by 45%, generates 30% [Life Steal], and doubles the DMG increase effect in [Trait] for each 1 NRG the target is missing, lasting for 4 turns. When the effect ends, ATK and DEF are reduced by 8%, lasting for 3 turns. Water of Exorcism Usable once, grants [Mana Ward] Increases DEF by 30%, gains [magical shield] equal to 150% of P.ATK; gains [Control Immunity] before being hit by magical attacks, lasting for 4 turns. When the effect ends, ATK and DEF are reduced by 8%, lasting for 3 turns. Estra is able to apply self-buff by using her [Potion] skill . The effects of both potions are extremely powerful when used together, but remember that each can only be used once per battle and lasts only 4 turns . Conditional Damage In order to reach Estra's full potential, it is necessary to maintain her Magical Shield, reduce enemy NRG, and increase Estra's relevant stats . Since attribute debuffs cannot be applied, be conscious of creating the perfect conditions for Estra by adjusting her gear, engravings, and party composition for each stage and enemy. Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Estra Aim to Reach 2★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 2★ → 5★ Each star upgrade increases Estra’s damage against enemies with low NRG. At 2 and 4-stars, she gains bonus physical attack against enemies with no NRG, while 3-star boosts her extra damage against targets whose magic attack is higher than her physical attack. These upgrades steadily improve her offensive power, so ultimately, you will want to reach 5-stars, where her skill cooldowns are further reduced by 1 turn. Priority Skills Skill Priority and Details Shadow Blitz ★★★★★ Move behind an enemy and perform a Basic Attack that has [Ignore Assist]. This powerful Skill allows you to attack again if you defeat the target. In stages with plenty of enemies, it enables free movement, so focus on boosting its damage and aim for consecutive takedowns. Thunder Arrow ★★★★★ Deals light damage, reduces the enemy’s NRG, and applies ✗Active Skills & ✗NRG Restored. This lets you trigger your Trait’s damage boost, making it a strong passive for increasing overall damage. Shadow Evasion ★★★★★ A powerful all-in-one Reaction with [Preempt] and action interruption. Note that it's ineffective against area attacks. Potion ★★★★★ A Choice Skill with strong self-buffing effects. Both options offer overwhelming power, so it’s usually worth including in your build. Since each can only be used once, consider swapping this skill with something else for longer battles. Spine Pierce ★★★★★ Deals high damage and bypasses Physical Shields when attacking from behind. This is usually the better option for a Basic Attack. No Restraints ★★★★★ Deals massive damage when the enemy's NRG is low. With the 5-star effect or cooldown reduction from allies, it can be used every turn. Prioritize [Shadow Blitz] for multi-target battles; this is better suited for single-target encounters. Shadow Step ★★★★ Though it has a long cooldown, it reduces enemy NRG and suppresses Skill use while boosting Trait damage. A good pick when Estra lacks NRG recovery support—include it when there is a clear need. Defense (Magical) ★★★★ Useful in stages or boss fights with AoE attacks where [Shadow Evasion] isn’t effective. Doom Decoy ★★★★ Useful when enemy buffs or self-debuffs are problematic. Also helpful when Estra can’t easily receive buffs from other characters, as it provides a self-buff option. Secondary Priorities Skill Build Priority and Details Stance of Exorcism ★★★ Has a high NRG cost, but is a good way to get Magical Shields. The shield from [Anti-Demon Potion] is quite sturdy already, so only include this skill in long battles or against enemies with high magic damage. Demon Hunting Trap ★★ Only include this when you absolutely need to interrupt a specific enemy's action. Hookshot ★★ Useful only when you need to pull in a troublesome enemy that [Shadow Blitz] can’t finish off. Feverish Attack ★ Low damage output, and since [Spine Pierce] already ignores Shields and is stronger overall, there’s little reason to use this. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Estra Void Stab Frostspine Grip Newborn Blade For Estra, two standout weapon choices are the [Void Stab], which focuses on damage by greatly boosting both critical rate and critical damage, and the well-rounded [Newborn Blade]. The latter increases HP and physical attack, raises the max value of her Magical Shield, and improves the amount recovered when using [Magic Suppressant Potion]. Both are excellent picks depending on how you want to build her. Recommended Trinkets for Estra Cage Mask Maverick's Cloak Shield of Justice For Estra, boosting her stats also increases both the strength of her Magical Shield and her damage output. That makes the [Maverick’s Cloak] a top pick for multi-target battles, while the [Cage Mask] is great for single-target encounters. Since she also has skills that deal direct damage, she pairs well with trinkets that help her finish off enemies using [Shadow Blitz]. Recommended Tarots for Estra Verdict of Justice The Fool's Naivety Temptation of The Devil Recommended choices for Estra include [Justice] to enhance her crits, [Fool], which increases both Max HP, which enhances her Magical Shield, and [Devil], which continuously boosts her potential. Recommended Engravings for Estra Resonance Effect Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Sword Sword When actively attacking [unharmed] enemies, increases Crit by 20% . Wand Wand After using a [basic attack], [skill], [strike back], or [assisting attack], gains 1 random [Level 2 Attribute Buff]. The [Sword + Wand] and [Sword + Pentacle] combinations are especially recommended for boosting stats, while [Wand + Wand] provides multiple buffs that can enhance Estra's overall performance. [Sword + Sword] is also a strong fit, making it a great choice for battles against multiple enemies. Estra's Skin Initial Ascended ▲ Unlock Ascended Skin when character reaches Star Level 5. Estra is a free-spirited hunter pursuing freedom. Unrestrained and carefree, Estra never plans for the future, nor worries about failure. To others, she seems to squander time and money with a nonchalant attitude toward everything. But only she knows the fiery rage of revenge that fuels her relentless self-improvement and mastery of her skills. After learning that her homeland was destroyed by Luvata , she swore to bring the sorceress to justice, avenging her family and the innocent lives lost. Estra's long quest for vengeance leads her to the swamps, yet upon finally reaching the place of truth, she finds herself facing upheaval of friendship and the collapse of her world.
