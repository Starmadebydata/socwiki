While under the effects of the Berserk State , Tristan deals the most amount of damage amongst all characters . Berserk State Details Berserk State Details 1 [Crimson Fury] Consumes 90% of current HP when activated, deals 50% damage to enemies within 2 squares, and enters Berserk State for 2 to 4 turns. (Changes depending on ★ level) 2 Act Again after transitioning to Berserk State, the CD of all skills is reset and NRG is fully recovered. You can only transition to Berserk State once per battle. 3 During Berserk State, Tristan is immune to Injured/Dying effects, as well as Disruption debuffs and Piercing DMG. 4 Increases damage dealt by +25% and reduces Healing Received by -80%. Furthermore, depending on the number of ★s, increases crit rate and crit damage by 0-20%. 5 Gains Unlimited Resolve and cannot be defeated for 1-2 turns, depending on Tristan's ★s. 6 At 5★, gains the additional effects of CD-2 turns for non-instant skills and -1 NRG cost. Max Damage Skill Rotation [Fervent Headbutt] : 70% → [Crimson Fury] : 50% → Transition to Berserk State (CD Reset & Act Again) → [Fervent Headbutt] : 70% → [Furious Pursuit] : 180% Total multipliers for 1 turn is 370% (including Berserk's 250% when in state) and can deal extremely high damage without support from allies . Unkillable for a Certain Number of Turns Once Tristan enters the Berserk State, she gains [Unlimited Resolve]. Similar to Alexei , this effect completely prevents Tristan from dying for 1-2 turns (dependent on ★s). Use Matching Gear and Passive Skills Because entering the Berserk State consumes 90% of Tristan's HP, you can reliably trigger the second passive effect of Brutal Axe and ignore a percentage of the target's DEF. Additionally, entering the Berserk State activates all of the effects of the [Blazing Blood] passive skill. Make sure to leverage Tristan's ability to lower her HP by equipping matching gear and passive skills. Negates Shields When Attacking Tristan's [Crimson Fury] trait allows her to dispel shields from her target in addition to dealing increased damage. Do note that there exists shields that cannot be removed, so be sure to differentiate between the two types of shields before positioning Tristan aggressively. Make Preparations for Berserk Window While Tristan deals immense amounts of damage while in her Berserk State, she is not particularly outstanding in her normal state. Prepare the battlefield by applying buffs/debuffs to other characters while adjusting their positions to allow Tristan to fully utilize her Berserk window . Star Level Up Priority & Recommended Stars for Tristan Aim to Reach 4★ → 5★ Star Level Up Priority Recommended Star Level 4★ → 5★ At 4-stars, [Unlimited Resolve] lasts for 2 turns, greatly improving Tristan’s overall usability—making it a key upgrade to aim for. The CD reduction and NRG cost decreases at 5-stars are nice bonuses, but can be somewhat covered by support from other characters or tactical skills, so only go for 5-star if you have the resources to spare. Once you've entered Berserk State, use [Fervent Headbutt] and [Furious Pursuit] to deal massive damage. Gear included in the build specialize in dealing as much single-target damage as possible. AoE Build Basic Attack Reaction Ascension Fatal Attack Street Style Blessing of Divine Shield Skill One-Punch Burial Controlled Demolition Blazing Blood Weapon Trinket Tarot Brutal Axe Blood Luxite Amulet Dream of The Magician This build emphasizes Tristan's AoE damage capabilities. Once you've entered Berserk State, use [One-Punch Burial] and [Controlled Demolition] to cause massive damage. By reducing the CD with 5-star traits and support from other characters, it is also possible to use [One-Punch Burial] again on a group of enemies already affected by Vulnerable II. Adjust Skills Appropriately If allies can inflict Vulnerable, or if you want to focus on consistent damage and agility over Piercing DMG, replace [Controlled Demolition] with [Explosive Luxite-Dust] or [Fervent Lunge]. Priority Skills Skill Build Priority and Details Blazing Blood ★★★★★ A powerful passive that adds up to 50% of Magic Attack to Physical Attack, with the full effect easily reached during the [Berserk State]. Since it plays a key role in boosting Tristan’s offensive power, it should always be equipped. Street Style ★★★★★ A Reaction that reduces damage from [Strike Back], [Preempt] attacks, and melee damage. Not only does it offer significant damage reduction, but it also counterattacks when hit by Strike Back, making it a strong, general Reaction. Fatal Attack ★★★★★ While Tristan won't often use Basic Attacks, this attack pairs well with Tristan thanks to her Trait, which boosts Crit Damage and Crit Rate. Furious Pursuit ★★★★ A Skill that deals high single-target damage at the cost of HP. When aiming to boost the effects of the "Brutal Axe" weapon and [Blazing Blood], the HP reduction can be seen as a benefit. Prioritize slotting this skill when focusing on single-target offense. Fervent Lunge ★★★★ An [Instant] skill moves, grants a buff, and immediately attacks—making it both easy to use and highly effective. Useful in both single-target and multi-target battles. One-Punch Burial ★★★★ Deals damage to enemies within 2 tiles after a knockback. As an area attack, this has high damage potential and can be used every turn if you have a way to reduce cooldown. Be mindful—the warp-to-ally effect has both advantages and drawbacks. Secondary Priorities Skill Priority and Details Explosive Luxite-Dust ★★★ An easy-to-use area attack Skill that covers a wide range and has no cooldown. It also applies a powerful [Lv2 Attribute Debuff], making it even more effective. A great choice when you need a reliable source of consistent damage. Controlled Demolition ★★★ When Tristan attacks, starts an action, or when an afflicted enemy is defeated, it deals Piercing DMG and applies a 20% increased damage taken debuff over a wide area. Since it’s Piercing DMG, the damage is essentially pure . Equip this when you want to inflict [Vulnerable II] across a large area. Knockback Pursuit ★★ Since the easy-to-use [Fervent Headbutt] also provides knockback, equip this only if you want two types of knockback or need a knockback option with 0 cooldown. Family ★★ Assisting Cover can be tricky to use, since it may cause Tristan or an ally to take unexpected hits. However, the wide-area buffs—Attack Up and damage reduction—are powerful, making it worth equipping in situations where those effects would be especially useful. Destructive Storm ★★ Though its damage is low, this Skill applies damage and debuffs over a very wide area. In stages where controlling an area and applying debuffs is more important than raw damage, it’s definitely worth equipping. Defense (Ranged Attack) ★ [Street Style] doesn’t work against Ranged Attacks, so equip this when need to reduce damage from Ranged attacks. Sealing Attack ★ This debuff cannot be nullified, making it effective against enemies with troublesome passives. However, since you'd usually want another character to remove passives instead of Tristan—who focuses on offense—this skill has a lower priority. Recommended Gear, Tarots, & Engravings Recommended Weapons for Tristan Brutal Axe Undying Flames Gale Sword The [Brutal Axe], which synergizes well with the HP-reducing [Berserk State], is the top recommendation. Other good choices include the [Tuning Hammer], which significantly boosts physical attack and can remove buffs, and the [Gale Sword], effective for prioritizing attacks after an [Act Again]. Recommended Trinkets for Tristan Maverick's Cloak Blood Luxite Amulet Shield of Justice For Tristan, trinkets that boost her offensive power during her [Berserk State] are highly recommended. The [Origin Hourglass] enhances the effect of [Blazing Blood], the [Blood Luxite Amulet] increases her AoE attack damage, while the [Berserk Incense] is a great pre-battle option to boost both movement and damage dealt. Recommended Tarots for Tristan Verdict of Justice Dream of The Magician Hierophant's Laws If you're building for single-target damage, equip [Hierophant]. For AoE-focused builds, go with [Magician]. Other strong choices include [Devil], which gives a big stat boost on stages with lots of enemies, and [Justice], which enhances your Critical rate for even more damage potential. Recommended Engravings for Tristan Resonance Effect Sword Cup When actively attacking, increases the DMG by 5% and grants 15% of [Life Steal]. Sword Wand Before actively attacking, for each 1 tile traversed, increases ATK and DEF by 4% , up to 12% . The effect lasts until the start of the next turn, Wand Wand After using a [basic attack], [skill], [strike back], or [assisting attack], gains 1 random [Level 2 Attribute Buff]. Engrave [Sword + Cup] or [Sword + Wand], both of which include [Sword] to easily increase [Attack] and activate an Engraving Harmonies that boost [Damage Dealt]. [Wand + Wand] is also a strong option, especially when paired with [Instant] Skills like [Fervent Lunge] and [Controlled Demolition]. Life Steal Can Reduce Potency While the healing from [Sword + Cup]’s [Life Steal] is excellent, but keep in mind that recovering HP can sometimes reduce the effectiveness of Skills like [Blazing Blood] and items like the [Blood Luxite Amulet].
